# DUALHEAD61 joint peak and RMS feasibility in the NF4 sham atlas

The directed search found a numerical continuous witness in **five of
16** exposed CAL worlds. Those five worlds passed the full rounded
four-arm head gate. Three also matched the active RMS within 0.005 in
both signs. The dual search generated numeric lower-bound estimates
and certified zero infeasible worlds at the registered joint limits.

| Panel | Continuous witnesses | Full rounded gate | Rounded RMS matches | Both-world RMS-matched roots |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Fit | 3/8 worlds | 3/8 worlds | 2/8 worlds | 0/4 |
| Disjoint CAL check | 2/8 worlds | 2/8 worlds | 1/8 world | 0/4 |

All 16 worlds qualified physically. The disjoint match's largest signed
RMS gap was 0.00337 and maximum vocabulary-logit shift was 0.11328.
Its two active–sham logit-shift cosines were −0.235 and −0.269.
Matching scalar RMS can leave a substantial difference in which
vocabulary logits move. The search had lower full-gate coverage than
HEADNULL60, which passed 11/16 worlds. Four worlds passed both searches.
The present result adds one disjoint RMS-matched world to the earlier
CAL observations.

The median numeric dual lower RMS bound was 0.01735 on fit and 0.01720
on check. The median RMS upper limits were 0.02318 and 0.02285. Exact
rational LDL and its outward float64 Gram allowance were exercised in
synthetic verification. No exact infeasibility witness was found for
the frozen atlas. Eleven continuous cases remain unresolved.
The search pertains to unit vectors in the same 24-dimensional stored
derivative atlas. The full-head results directly measure the rounded
BF16 interventions.

Both CAL panels were previously exposed. The run streamed all 166,144
vocabulary rows for each of 80 unique clean or edited head states. It
used 461 MiB peak sampled private memory with no new pretrained decoder
forwards, sealed ARG-03 access, or formal inference. The reusable
spectral proposal is bound by hash in the aggregate. The first attempt
stopped at a mismatched config key before writing panel results; both
panels were replayed after repairing and resealing the source.
