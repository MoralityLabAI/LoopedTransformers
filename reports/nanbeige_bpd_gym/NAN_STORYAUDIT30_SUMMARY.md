# Native NF4 storyworld development: competence remains low

All 72 native story trajectories were independently audited. On
the 18 DEV main worlds from nine source roots, final-room accuracy was **1/18**
and complete final/checkpoint/path trace correctness was **0/18**. This
condition does not establish clean story-task competence for BPD donor transfer.

| DEV arm | Worlds | Source roots | Valid format | Final correct | All checkpoints correct | Path correct | Joint correct |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Main | 18 | 9 | 18 | 1 | 0 | 1 | 0 |
| Complete-trace copy | 18 | 9 | 18 | 14 | 5 | 14 | 5 |
| Whole-story erased | 18 | 9 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

Main and copy replies all satisfied the unchanged bounded packet grammar.
The main floor therefore persists among well-formed replies. Copy controls
explicitly supplied the entire verified trace, yet their joint correctness
was 5/18, below final-only 14/18. Final correctness cannot rescue wrong
checkpoints or paths. Erased replies count as format failures under the
original rules; these are preserved outcomes.

Both counterfactual worlds were jointly correct in 0/9 main roots and 2/9
copy roots. The arms reuse those same nine roots.

CAL remains separate: main final/joint 0/6 and 0/6; copy final/joint 5/6 and 4/6;
erased 0/6. Those arms repeat the same three CAL roots. DEV has actual, first-order
recorded-belief and nested recorded-belief queries at 32/64/96 events. The
complete report retains every query/event-count/arm/split and all five
endpoints. No source, missing trajectory or difficult case was dropped.

The audit checked **3,990 generated prefixes and 159,600 physical vectors**, all
40 loop/layer/site coordinates per prefix, across all 72 trajectories with zero missing.
It verified source oracles, pinned prompt/token histories, decoding/stops,
closed-record/archive hashes, physical widths/finite values, native-write
identity and paired batch/padding. It used **108.1 MiB maximum sampled private
RAM**, retained at least **4.946 GiB available** across 403 samples, ran one
thread at Below Normal priority, and imported no Torch or model. These are
sampled resources. No neural response was replayed.

A separate post-audit JSON reconciliation matched all 180 endpoint cells,
world/root coverage bounds, source IDs and private closed-summary totals.
It used 21.6 MiB maximum sampled private RAM and retained 4.222 GiB available,
with no NumPy, Torch, state-array reads or regrading. Its administrative lease
was capped at 64 MiB, with 4.25 GiB admission and a 4 GiB reserve; the original
audit's 0.75 GiB/5 GiB/4 GiB limits and grades are unchanged. Earlier metadata
admission refusals are retained in provenance.

This is a separate early packet/state audit of the still-running RAM28
condition. **Final model-parameter verification remains pending** until its
