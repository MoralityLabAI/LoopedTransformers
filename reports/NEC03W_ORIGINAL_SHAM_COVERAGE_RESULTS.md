## Scientific implication and stop

The source-only coherence test produced some promising individual-world correctness differences under the old strict null (roots4/7), yet only a minority of composed-case comparisons satisfied the full original angle and cohesion requirements. The original **+1.2207pp**, p=0.50 and failed resolution gates remain the causal developmental result. The new archive analysis is **retrospective coverage accounting**, with no new intervention or independent-root behavioral inference.

A future source-only experiment could preregister a physical **subspace-level control** that searches a richer set of matched directions, with an explicit output-L2 dose and source-group coherence contract, and whose objective is to raise root-wise control availability while keeping a meaningful scientific null. A new source-only group panel such as original training groups **40..47** would require a separate frozen source-policy lock before scores. It must retain all eight original roots, both teacher arms, the original source-ineligible ITT zeros, a root-level effect and uncertainty plan, the +2pp meaningful floor and independent auditors. Its external or confirmation results cannot be inferred from the present retrospective grid.
