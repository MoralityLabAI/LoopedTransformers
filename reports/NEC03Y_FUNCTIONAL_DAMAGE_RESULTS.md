A revealing archived example is **dev5-001 additive**. All 256 original continuous shams pass the full native physical-matching contract and have source/control output-vector cosine at least **0.75**. The sham still causes 96 formerly correct source cases to be wrong. Of these 96, **80** occurred when the worst archived gradient-angle gap across selected visits was below **0.05**, and **16** were in the [.15,.25] gradient-angle bin. Among cases whose minimum count of qualified candidate sham supports across visits was **≥200**, no original correctly scored input was damaged; when minimum candidate counts were **50–199**, 49 such cases were damaged, and for **1–49**, 47 cases were damaged. These exploratory in-sample patterns identify sensitivity to physical control availability and selection without establishing a causal statistical effect of support count.

The output-vector cosine alone therefore does not certify that a disjoint active-code sham is functionally inert for the target task. A physically close match can disrupt the same semantic computational content as the source removed, particularly in saturated additive teachers.

## Scientific disposition

The NEC03X continuous nonnegative-coefficient disjoint physical sham improved original source physical-control availability compared with NEC03V/W's discrete active-code null. The current archived damage audit shows that this *matched* null can itself destroy a substantial fraction of previously correct source-trained task behavior, including **96/256 additive cases in one original qualified world**, independent of any resulting source-minus-sham correctness difference. Combined with the original NEC03X **negative, root-heterogeneous source-specific necessity effect**, failed ≥205/256 matching coverage in many qualified teacher arms and failed root-level uncertainty gates, the existing **continuous sham family should be retired as a functionally inert confirmation control for these five-input task models**.

This conclusion is limited to the previously trained original source contexts and intervention family. It does not prove semantic equivalence of source and sham circuits, a lack of interpretable SAE subspaces, or a population-level mechanism result. Any subsequent causal attempt needs an independently registered **functional-inertness criterion** and a task family with enough non-saturated correctness headroom, while preserving a scientifically meaningful physical-null contract. Developmental artifact archiving and redesign are appropriate next steps; the recruited population stays protected.
