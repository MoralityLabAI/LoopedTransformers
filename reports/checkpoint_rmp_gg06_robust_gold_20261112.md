## Sharp empirical-support TV theorem

For one fixed gold scoring panel of \(n=128\) native measured per-sample consequences \(d_i=\ell_i(\theta_X)-\ell_i(\theta_C)\), consider
\[
\mathcal W_\rho=\{w_i\ge0,\;\sum_iw_i=1,\;TV(w,\mathbf u)\le\rho\},
\quad \mathbf u=(1/n,\ldots,1/n).
\]

**GG-06/T1.** At each registered \(\rho\in\{0,1/64,1/16\}\), the sharp interval of empirical-gold harm \(\sum_iw_id_i\) is attained by greedy probability transport from the highest-effect nominally weighted samples to a smallest-effect sample for the minimum, and the reverse for the maximum. This holds with the registered \(n\) and radius because the destination can receive the full \(\rho\) mass and the donor list can supply it, up to the point where further transfer would have zero benefit. The value of the optimized linear functional is achieved by an explicit nonnegative, normalized weight vector whose TV distance equals its transport mass. The independent exact-rational grid oracle verifies each finite-dimensional instance of this algorithm on small cases. Optimization is exact **conditional on the measured float values**, with ordinary numerical reproducibility limits for the neural computation.

We call a query **robust harmful** if all eight gold-panel lower bounds are strictly above \(10^{-7}\), **robust beneficial** if all eight upper bounds are below \(-10^{-7}\), and **unresolved** otherwise. This is a *finite empirical-support* ambiguity set. No guarantee about gold examples absent from these sampled panels is implied.

## Paired independent-panel depth disagreements

Each independent four-cue gold panel was compared descriptively with the six-cue panel of the same panel-index, using **separate** RNG streams. The number of source queries with opposite signs out of 48, across indices 0–7, was:

- **Seed 11:** \([13,10,21,14,13,20,0,19]\). The reversal rate across the complete 384 query×panel-index comparisons is **110/384 = 28.65%**. **37/48** queries reverse sign on at least one of eight comparisons; **0/48** reverse on all eight.
- **Seed 17:** \([19,32,31,20,18,18,20,20]\). Total **178/384 = 46.35%**. **46/48** queries reverse in at least one comparison; **0/48** reverse on all eight.

One pair of independently sampled panels produces zero depth reversals for seed 11, while another yields 21. These are *scoring panels*, not alternative physical SGD actions: both task-model successor weights are held fixed across every panel evaluation.

The earlier GG-05 result of **zero cross-depth reversals on its specific two independently drawn panels** remains valid for those panels, while GG-06 shows that other independently sampled gold panels can recover sign disagreements. GG-05P's two pivotal historical cases highlighted a severe version of the same scoring-sensitivity mechanism.

## Baseline-CE hard-case weighting

For every panel, the auditor selects the **seven highest baseline-loss examples before any report update**, then weights those seven by a fixed multiplier \(\tau\in\{1,4,16\}\) and renormalizes. The selected hard-case indices do not use report effects or heldout controller actions.

| Hard-case factor | Seed 11 four-cue sign changes | Seed 17 four-cue | Seed 11 six-cue | Seed 17 six-cue |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| \(\tau=1\), ordinary uniform scoring | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| \(\tau=4\) | 0 | 0 | **11** | **2** |
| \(\tau=16\) | **2** | 0 | **17** | **4** |

These counts are **query×panel signed changes out of 384** relative to nominal scoring. At multiplier 16, the seven examples receive \(112/233\approx48.07\%\) of each panel's total scoring weight. This intentionally stress-tests heavy-tail gold objectives. As with TV reweighting, it does not imply those weights are calibrated deployment probabilities.

## Interpretation and next experiment

The physical training-update effect is stable when judged by the four-cue gold task on the registered 128-example panels. The same updates have substantially more uncertain signs on six-cue gold samples. The independent eight-panel experiment therefore clarifies why the original two-example GG-05P forensic result mattered: **rare, difficult gold examples and scorer composition affect both the magnitude and sign of estimated future-training harm**.

The next study should test a *preregistered protected-tail objective* using a fresh independent task grammar and enough rare-hard examples to estimate sign stability without opportunistically selecting influential historical cases. A fixed gold-distribution mixture, importance-weight confidence bounds, and an explicit out-of-support stress suite would make empirical safety claims more interpretable.

This study does **not** test a trained model inferring its own optimizer, strategically hiding training awareness, changing its own controller weights, or bargaining with a hypothetical future simulator. It measures task-specific changes to actual cloned neural weights and a finite family of independently selected synthetic gold-scoring distributions.
