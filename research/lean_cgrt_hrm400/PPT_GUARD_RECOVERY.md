# Paid guard checks with native witness recovery

The previous guard fork had a fixed continuation. A spare Lean call was sufficient when one ground check identified the witness. This pilot puts proof search after the witness opening under policy control. The pending predicate is shared by both guard goals through the existential witness. Choosing the wrong witness can still lead to a proof if the policy detects the failing guard early and has enough calls left to restore the fork.

## Native registration

Use 48 source pairs and 40 separate development pairs, balanced between the original BPD recurrence and the audited MathCan mask operators. Reserve the deeper computation terms for development. Each branch opens two real Lean guard goals and one pending predicate. One branch has two true guards. Exactly one of the other branch's guards fails. Swap the full branch guards in the paired world and vary the position of the failing guard. Verify each of the four possible probes and both orders of trying the two guards in Lean. Independently compile both successful goal-order scripts. Each canonical success consumes six charged tactic calls including the shared setup. A paid probe checks a single visible guard on the saved fork without advancing the retained proof. Goal selection and restoring an already saved snapshot are administrative operations. Only the chosen native tactic or guard evaluation spends a call. Use the same maximum of eight charged calls as the earlier guard pilot.

Test total budgets of six, seven and eight on every world. At the fork the policy may enter a witness branch or probe a visible guard. A successful in-branch check persists in the proof state. A rejected one consumes its call, exposes the failed guard, and restores the fork. The policy may then enter the other branch with its remaining allowance. Cap failed and successful calls together. Register a zero-value stop option for the fitted and model-based policies.

## Matched learning and controls

Log 24 source-only exploratory tapes per world and budget by replaying the native-verified atomic outcomes. The tape mixtures use random exploration, fixed preflight and direct branch entry. The Bellman and empirical fits use the same checked transitions. Bellman links recorded transitions at the same observable recovery state and the empirical arm receives the suffix returns from the original behavior tapes. Keep the shared actor initialization and action sampler. Compare against full-history TRM with the same Bellman targets, frozen ridge calibration and a direct supervised guard-probability planner that evaluates the budgeted decision tree. Keep the preregistered preflight rule and exact arithmetic fold oracle as separate controls. The common action features carry typed guards, current proof checks, paid observations, frozen direct guard probabilities, and the audited four-source tactic curves from MathCan, EvalMath, LoopedTransformers and BPD.

The primary descriptive endpoint is utility averaged by root across worlds and budgets. Give each joined proof one unit and subtract 0.025 per charged native call. Report paired certifications separately at each budget, verified recoveries, and the gain over each fitted and calibrated comparator. Require +0.025 root-macro utility over every matched fitted or calibrated comparator. To test the use of paid native evidence, flip the first paid probe bit only at the following decision. Preserve the physical guard outcomes and the real action menu. Verify the charged prefix and independently compile all selected successful scripts. This is a development-only test under `FSI-01 REDESIGN_OR_PILOT_ONLY`.

The post hoc budget diagnosis keeps the fitted weights fixed and gives every arm the same public completion mask. Entering a witness needs at least four remaining calls at the fork. A probe needs one more. After entering, each outstanding guard and the final close need one call each. This mask uses the public proof shape, and the native guard outcomes remain hidden until their checks. Keep the original unmasked scores and the rescored results separately.
