# Native computational guard forks

The supervised Horn meter matched the continuation ceiling in the previous pilot. This follow-up tests a public tactic menu whose transitions create computational side conditions and instantiate a shared existential witness. The Lean kernel decides the conditions. A rule matcher that reads binding and arity alone gets the same immediate progress outcome on both openings.

## Registration

Use the original BPD `OptimalBasis.geom` and audited MathCan training-only `FiniteHorizonGap.maskAt` definitions in Lean. Construct 48 source pairs and 40 development pairs with disjoint seed streams. Balance both imported functions and seven- and eight-tactic proof lengths. Reserve deeper computation inputs for development. Open one local hypothesis to instantiate the witness, discharge its computational side goals and use that same witness in the pending predicate. Swap the last computational condition between the two hypotheses in the paired world. Both openings make progress, both early conditions are true in the eight-step profile, and the wrong branch fails only on its final condition. The Python fold and mask reference proposes the labels. Native execution and standalone Lean proofs verify them, including a refutation of every distinct false ground guard.

Keep the registered native menu of witness construction, hypothesis applications and ground `decide` calls. Each issued tactic, including a failed diagnostic, counts against the eight-check allowance. Record the deliberately restricted palette so its endpoints describe this action budget. The immediate-progress control uses a fixed tie order. The paid-native-probe control tries to establish the first discriminating guard at the saved witness fork and takes the other branch if Lean rejects it. It uses that extra check only in the seven-step profile. Both controls use the same typed continuation. The full host fold and mask evaluator provides the reference witness, which is checked independently in Lean.

Collect source-only one- and two-tactic fragments from already visited snapshots. Strip reference scripts and winning labels from the source interface. Keep exact state-hash joins, censored within-tape suffixes, and Bellman bounds separately. Do not use the development witness traces for later fitting. Report native progress that does not discriminate branches, individual verified proofs, complete root pairs, the paid-check structural control, and the number of compiled source proofs assembled without a successful root tape. This dataset is a computational stress test under `FSI-01 REDESIGN_OR_PILOT_ONLY`.

Run each guard tactic with the same finite Lean recursion allowance of 8,192. The standalone false-guard checks and the paid probe use the same allowance.

## Matched fork fit

Fit the recurrent two-slot actor to backed-up intervals or to within-tape suffixes with identical initialization and source action batches. The TRM receives the same visible input tokens and Bellman labels. Freeze a ridge calibration on the exact backed-up source labels. Include a direct supervised reader trained to predict success from the actual ground guard checks. It receives the last visible guard of each candidate when comparing the two openings. The common recurrent features contain raw arithmetic operands, the last and earlier guard roles, proof context and the cached four-source tactic curves. No host fold values are features. Train all heads for 480 updates of 32 source action rows, or the same update and batch count on deduplicated guard outcomes for the direct reader.

At scoring each actor chooses the witness hypothesis once. The registered typed continuation spends the remaining Lean budget and receives no free paid guard check. The separate paid native control retains its original budgeted probe. Treat complete verified pairs as the primary endpoint. Require three additional complete pairs over the strongest matched trained and calibrated control for a meaningful pilot gain. Report the exact fold oracle separately. To test sensitivity to the discriminating condition, swap just the last-guard input between the two candidates before their live Lean continuations. This is a mechanism diagnostic on a development cohort previously opened for the native structural audit.

## Results

Both hypotheses advanced on all 80 development worlds. On the wrong branch every early guard passed, followed by a checked rejection at the final guard. Lean independently compiled 196 successful scripts across the structural study, including 20 successful paid-probe scripts, and 88 distinct false-guard refutations. The checked short source traces stitched into all 96 source proofs. Their 1,248 decision-action labels included 532 observed suffix labels, 96 positive Bellman labels at the root and another 96 at the witness fork. There were no positive within-tape suffix labels at either location.

Bellman added four complete pairs over suffix fitting at the same individual proof count. It trailed TRM, ridge and the direct guard reader on the registered paired metric. The paid native probe solved every pair in the seven-tactic profile, which had one spare call. The eight-tactic profile left no room for that probe. The direct reader completed 12 out of 20 of those tight pairs. Swapping the actor's last-guard inputs changed 56 selected witnesses and increased the Bellman result by two complete pairs. This intervention shows that the score depends on the guard representation and leaves the accuracy of its binding unresolved.

The warm prior retained its 459 audited decisions from MathCan, EvalMath, LoopedTransformers and BPD. The direct guard reader saw 137 distinct positive checks and 48 distinct rejected checks. The exact oracle using both arithmetic folds remained sufficient. Both public operators would require semantic execution or stronger learned generalization at the reserved depths.
