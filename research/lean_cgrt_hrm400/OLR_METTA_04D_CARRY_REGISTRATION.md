The historical majority reference also has a sign inversion. The feature encoder uses 2*vote-1, where vote 0 selects r0 and vote 1 selects r1. A positive weighted vote ought to prefer r1. ## Frozen intervention arms

Each of the 3 original loop3 checkpoints is evaluated in three modes, all with the same architecture, three tied attention visits per decision and identical public features:

1. natural: ordinary carried vector.
2. zero: replace the previous decision's carry by a zero vector before each later decision (same model code, tensor shape and compute).
3. sham: add a deterministic pseudo-random Rademacher direction to the previous carry, normalized to the L2 norm of that carry. This matches the norm of the perturbation used by the zero arm. The sham seed is a hash of root ID, model seed and decision number. It does not read labels or outcomes beyond the policy's ordinary public state. Record dose and decision metadata.

All three share an unperturbed first decision, which must match the frozen root action. Compare the paired natural-minus-zero effect with natural-minus-sham. This is a trajectory intervention, not a logit-margin-matched local probe; downstream states and call costs may diverge and are recorded. Equal visits per decision do not imply equal total inference FLOPs when the policies visit different numbers of stages.

Additional frozen comparisons: original one-pass and direct models; fixed-r0 with native restore and repair; corrected signed weighted majority; source-calibrated log-likelihood reference (per public strength-pattern and claim position, Laplace smoothing from the original TRAIN labels, no new outcomes); full-information exact planner. All reference controllers probe the guard first, restore at most once only if a four-check eligible continuation fits, and use the same public macro eligibility to choose the two-step or manual tail. No reference uses numerical truth except the separately labelled privileged exact planner. The source-calibrated comparator explicitly assumes conditional independence of admitted votes given branch and public strength pattern. It is a strong simple control, not certified posterior calibration.
