# Paid Lean quotes bound to typed proof rules

The prior learned quote policy tied TRM and a lookup calibration. Its scores failed when the same tactics changed display slots. This pilot links each paid observation to literal Lean tactics and counts their occurrences in each proof plan. Each scored theorem has two independently certified plans and a fixed allowance. The benchmark meters synthetic tariff effort on the actual Lean steps.

## Cohort and mechanism

Freeze 48 fresh source and 40 further development Horn roots, excluding the earlier budget, public price and private quote roots. Source quotes target rules or facts that occur in a single plan. Every development root offers one quote on a shared downstream tactic. The planner must propagate its charge to both plans, including after permuting the display order of the plans. Randomize the carrier identities, quote channels, and slot order. Keep initial producer prices equal and bind the resulting literal tactics in the registry.

The environment has two private binary flags. Their paid quotes reveal the actual low or high tariff on their own tactics. A third carries a banded price and has an inverse-linked counterpart in the other plan. The joint price process is learned from source feedback. The policy sees the two literal occurrences for each quoted rule and any counterpart, arity, band type, current public allowance, and fees. It receives binary observations only after payment. Five regimes probe unaffordable diagnosis, direct purchase, conditional multi-quote purchase, and the option to commit without acquisition. The generator is available to an analytical ceiling. The fitted arms receive the same public inputs and fragment outcomes.

Register both complete plans through existing Lean kernel replay and independent compilation. Log source observations on short tapes cut before inspecting their outcomes. Freeze linked Bellman and empirical return targets under the same observed source transitions. Development summaries average by root over the five regimes and four private worlds. The descriptive gate is at least +0.025 proof-minus-quote-cost utility over all matched learned and calibration controls. Retain the proof rate and all failed paths. Compare the exact policy to one-step acquisition to determine whether the task has room for multi-step information gathering. Record both paid-bit and presentation interventions. The study is a developmental mechanism test under `FSI-01 REDESIGN_OR_PILOT_ONLY`.

## Matched fitting

The rule-bound recurrence learns a shared local tariff function and a joint quote-word distribution. Under each hypothetical quote word it bills the two plan slots through the literal rule occurrences, including inverse-linked tariffs and shared charges. The masked belief state mixes commitment probabilities for the remaining allowance and backs up acquisition at the public quote fee. A small typed-plan residual reads the same frozen four-source Lean prior provided to TRM and calibration. Test whether both plan columns and their predictions permute together. Also swap the rule links alone while leaving the true charged rules, physical plans, and paid evidence fixed.

Train three matched copies with 640 source batches of 16 decisions. One uses linked Bellman returns, one uses within-fragment suffix returns, and one fits commitment success alone before using the same inference-time planner. All receive the same next-bit forecasts from observed source feedback during fitting. The commitment-only arm tests whether learning prices and then planning is already sufficient. Compare them with the unfitted recurrence, a full-history TRM trained on Bellman returns, and frozen ridge calibration with public rule links and their observed-bit products. The model checkpoints freeze before policy scoring. The analytical ceiling receives the generator's actual tariff formula. No policy receives that formula in the fit.

## Results

The source registry contains 26,416 fragments and 3,004 public decision states. Backing up observed transitions supplied 370 quote targets whose individual fragments had no observed terminal suffix. The prior carried 459 audited decisions from MathCan, EvalMath, LoopedTransformers and BPD. Each rule head had 67 trainable parameters and the comparison TRM had 10,394. The fit checkpoint SHA-256 was `f28f95b1fdabe37d0cc177bce2fea5ee31ed949646003083f6445b5194f12340`.

The Bellman arm gained 26 certificates and 0.062188 utility over TRM, using 380 fewer units of quote effort. It gained 152 certificates and 0.223203 utility over frozen ridge. Both utility comparisons favored it on all 40 roots. The additional certificates over TRM came entirely from the expensive-reconciliation regime. Bellman matched the exact policy's proof count and spent 92 more quote units, all in the overlap regime.

Bellman gained 24 certificates and 0.015938 utility over the matched empirical-suffix fit. The commitment-only fit reached the same proof count with 24 fewer quote units and 0.001875 higher utility. The descriptive floor over every matched control was missed. The result favors the learned rule-bound planner on this task and leaves the incremental value of Bellman training unresolved against supervised commitment fitting.

## Binding and paid-observation interventions

Changing presentation preserved every physical Lean trace and outcome for each rule recurrence. TRM fell from 587 to 101 proofs under the same reordered inputs. Swapping the inferred rule-to-plan links alone in the Bellman arm, while preserving the actual prices and displayed physical plans, reduced 613 proofs to 116 with the same total quote effort. A matched paid-bit donor changed 524 of 548 eligible Bellman commitments. Those 548 cases contained 513 natural proofs and 32 after the donor intervention. The paid prefix and charge through the targeted quote were identical, and every equal-observation sham replayed identically.

The held roots contain a shared priced tactic on every layout. They also all have conjunctive goals, versus 16 of 48 source roots. The slot reorder and link swap compare the same theorem and prices, while the overall source-to-development comparison includes both shifts. The evidence stays at `FSI-01 REDESIGN_OR_PILOT_ONLY`. A test of the incremental training objective needs sparse native continuations where commitment outcomes alone provide weaker coverage.

Lean independently compiled 176 plans at registry freeze and 80 completed scripts at evaluation. The fitted comparison and interventions replayed 395 unique prefixes and 2,087 native tactic steps. Each plan uses at most eight tactics. All 89 native-enabled tests passed. Collection, fitting and scoring ran on logical CPU 2 under the 2 GiB process-tree cap. The highest observed committed memory was 0.80 GiB during collection.
