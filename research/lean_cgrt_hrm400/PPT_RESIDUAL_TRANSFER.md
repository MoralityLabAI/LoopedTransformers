The fitted candidate uses the typed proof slots of Plan A and the tied, gated cross-pass messages of Plan B. Its fitted head and recurrent block have 7,369 trainable parameters. The TRM control has 10,393, with both sharing the same frozen prior. The reward fit derived current-policy backups from native source transitions. The same initial two-slot weights in the imitation arm received optimistic exact source targets. The archived fit uses six BPD, six LoopedTransformers, two EvalMath and one MathCan source roots. The transfer consumed the frozen weights and stopping rules.

The repaired prospective comparison clears the scoped objective on directed BPD proof recovery. It establishes a proof gain over TRM and frozen calibration and an efficiency gain over same-model imitation for this checkpoint. The result is single-seed and task-specific. It leaves the broader gym's online fast-weight experiments and a confirmatory mechanism comparison as separate research questions.

The repaired replay checked 45 selected traces and independently compiled 90 distinct scored scripts. The original menu-mismatch rerun checked 45 traces and independently compiled 64 scored scripts, reproducing its initial printed scores. Its directed panel remained limited to two reachable proofs.
