## Inference semantics

A directed triple (i,k,j) performs the exact Boolean revision R_ij ← R_ij ∩ (R_ik ∘ R_kj). Any schedule preserves every feasible assignment. A terminal table fixed under all triples is the greatest fixed subrelation of the initial table; any two such terminating schedules give the same table. A target shrinking cannot invalidate a previously satisfied revision unless an operand changes. Enqueue every consumer of a changed operand, including repeated indices. No-op screening: relative to initial unary domains, if one operand is the full endpoint product and the other covers all required opposite endpoints, their composition is the full endpoint product. Skip that triple until a relevant operand changes. A sparse queue still permits dense fill-in, with cubic worst-case initial enumeration and dependencies. Empty relation proves inconsistency. Budget stops leave a sound overapproximation, not necessarily a fixed point. Lean proves abstract monotonicity, preservation, safe skipping and conditional schedule-independent terminal exactness; queue and bitset refinement tested rather than formally extracted.

Independent audit: 96 fresh n=4 systems (seed 2026112550), enumerate all 256 assignments and compare full fixed tables of five schedules with independent synchronous closure; for inconsistent tables compare contradiction status. Assert each feasible assignment's pair entries survive every step. K4/3-color and K5/4-color are explicit higher-order obstructions: path consistency unchanged, exhaustive worlds empty, DSATUR refutes. No claim of general completeness.
