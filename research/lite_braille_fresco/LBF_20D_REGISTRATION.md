# LBF-20D registration: adaptive premise selection under correlation

Parent 90b9f071 (merged LBF-20C). Development-only. No sealed cohort or confirmatory claim.

## Decision model

For a horizon-filtered Pareto frontier, a monotone DNF over guard IDs describes proof feasibility. Remove strict superset masks for the Boolean query objective; keep original witness paths for proof generation. A finite latent mixture specifies a joint law over fixed guard verdicts, with independent Bernoulli guards conditional on latent component z. For observed successful S and failed F, compute posterior mixture weights proportional to prior(z) times products of p_zg on S and (1-p_zg) on F. Then q_g(S,F) is the posterior marginal. Terminal success iff some mask is a subset of S; terminal portfolio failure iff every mask intersects F. With unit query costs, minimize 1 + q_g V(S+g,F) + (1-q_g)V(S,F+g). Since each query increases the observed set, backward induction gives exact model-optimal expected cost. Permit every unqueried variable in the synthetic joint DP, including diagnostic queries outside the currently feasible DNF. For an independent model restrict to currently relevant guards. The native correlated approximation is deliberately restricted to surviving relevant guards to bound planning; it is not the globally optimal joint policy. No claim that portfolio rejection proves a premise false.

## Synthetic exact and learning study

- 128 independently generated 6-guard mixture problems; cross-check exact optimal expectation by integrating over every complete world and replaying the optimal decision tree. Compare independent DP, restricted joint DP, posterior-aware risk, marginal risk under the same true joint law.
- Separately generate 96 train / 32 dev (6 guards) / 12 stress (8 guards) DNF roots. Reject repeated permutation-invariant signatures across cohorts, ensuring different structural isomorphism classes; not a holdout from all mathematical families. Priors are known to every policy. Train on at most 128 decision states per train root, using exact all-query joint Q-value regret labels. Fixed feature encoding includes DNF, both mixture components, prior weight, observed S/F and valid guard mask. No dev tuning. All three models receive identical input and output labels. Hidden width 64, residual tanh linear block, direct1 and recur3 share parameter count, untied3 matches three recurrent block evaluations with more parameters. Three seeds (107,109,113); 400 AdamW steps, batch 128, lr .002, weight decay .0001, matched draws. Regress action Q regrets, choose smallest valid prediction. Evaluate each learned policy's *exact* expected query count under the true joint law, not Monte Carlo; root and seed summaries, state-level local regret descriptive only. Include analytic baselines. Save checkpoint digests and all fit receipts. A negative recurrence finding is acceptable. These models do not observe Lean proof states and are not native controllers.

## Native study (same static guard factoring as 20C)

36 exposed roots from 20C, plus 6 fresh paired template roots under two public contexts (hp:P vs hp:Q); 48 total episodes per arm, 192 total. The 12 paired-context cases are six independent templates; do not count as twelve independent families. Guards are still static propositions in a context, not arbitrary state-dependent rewrite rules. No native fitting; verify the LBF-20B frozen ranker SHA-256 from 20C.

Arms: original Pareto risk/path-first; exact independent DP under frozen marginal prior restricted to surviving masks; common-mode mixture DP with same marginals, two equiprobable components p +/- .8 min(p,1-p), restricted queries (an uncalibrated heuristic); context-aware risk control boosts to .99 exact public atomic hypotheses from the native context. All use the same guard-text cache, actual Lean outcomes, shortest ready witness, 24 calls, 4s soft cap, H=20. Rotate arms by root. Measure actual costs including planning and final kernel, no time-normalization of unfinished searches. The query model does not account for internal tactic or proof term complexity; measured wall time is separate. Prior is not guaranteed calibrated on native contexts.

After all four arms on a root finish, independently query each guard in the original context; compare to observed cached-context verdicts. Archive the additional audit calls separately, never feed verdicts to a controller. This measures some effects of expanding local proof contexts; no claim of general context-invariance. Final native proofs independently compile in Lean 4.19.0 with only permitted axioms, no sorryAx. Archive all trajectories, checkpoints, generator specs, and outcome counts.

Engineering gate: complete both runs with explicit outcomes, exact finite audit, standalone certificate counts and allowed axioms. No binary superiority gate.
