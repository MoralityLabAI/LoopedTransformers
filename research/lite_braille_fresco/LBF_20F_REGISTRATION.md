# LBF-20F registration: shared witness CSPs, relational recurrence and exact controls

Parent 6b6af229 (LBF-20E). Development-only CPU study. Freeze source, generator and budgets before execution. No sealed cohorts, no claim of general Lean superiority.

## Domain and structural obstruction

Finite assignments x_i in {0,1,2,3}; unary domains and public binary relation facts. Joint witness assignments must satisfy every constraint. The parity-ring generator is a disjoint union of two 2-lifts with random per-variable value permutations, even and odd total parity. All values pass arc consistency; only the even mode is viable (some synthetic roots deliberately make both modes inconsistent). A uniform-feature, row-normalized value-message network on these regular lifts has identical initial embeddings and identical aggregated messages at every node/value. By induction the shared and untied updates keep them identical, regardless of depth. This is an architectural limitation, not a lower bound on all graph neural nets. Path consistency composes binary relations, so it can detect the contradictory parity cycle.

Lean formalizes arc/path preservation of satisfying assignments and the Boolean odd-cycle/local-support example; no executable extraction or general completeness claim. Independent audit: 96 four-variable systems, exhaustive 4^4 assignments per root, exact support checks and zero false negatives of AC/PC. Additional 32 satisfiable parity rings over n=6,8,10,12: AC unchanged, PC unary domains exactly match feasible support.
