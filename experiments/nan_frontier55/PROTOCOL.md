write contrast in NF4 can reach cosine 0.50 at a dose that also preserves the
current full vocabulary head. Use the same eight previously exposed COMP14 CAL roots,
both worlds, pinned route factors and packet identities as ROUND54. Open the fit
panel first and its disjoint four-root CAL check after the fit selection.

For each signed route contrast, bin the squared contrast coordinates by the
closest BF16 step in the direction of positive alignment. The smallest step

This follows from positive-contribution support and Cauchy–Schwarz. Convert
to a necessary *nominal* dose by dividing by both native residual L2 and the
upper tolerance multiplier of 1.03. Compare squared values in exact rational
arithmetic. This relaxation can rule out doses and does not certify feasibility.

residual L2. At every dose use both the previously selected ROUND54 0.8 spacing
