# NAN BPD 01 research context and evidence limits

The four repository heads and the ARG-02 adapter branch are pinned in
[SOURCE_MANIFEST.json](SOURCE_MANIFEST.json). This campaign uses one frozen
Nanbeige checkpoint. Positive synthetic results motivate controls; they do not
establish a mechanism in this pretrained model.

## Donor transfer and gauge controls

[BPD CQ-03](https://github.com/MoralityLabAI/BPD/pull/20) trains a physical
synthetic recurrence before decomposition and reports donor transfer, including
failed seed gates. [CQ-05](https://github.com/MoralityLabAI/BPD/pull/31) strengthens
the comparison with feedback from the actual reached state, static masks,
random components, retained zero-dose pairs, and canonicalized factor gauges.
Its synthetic results motivate reached-state routing and physical norm matching
here. The controller must not observe a cached clean hidden trajectory.

[CQ-04](https://github.com/MoralityLabAI/BPD/pull/25) also reports strong
synthetic input-adaptive allocation, but only 67/1024 donor pairs satisfy its
eligibility gate. Its 67/67 conditional transfer is not an unconditional
1024-pair result. The controller sees inputs before routing, whereas CQ-05
tests feedback from reached states. Both distinctions are retained here.

PD-09/10 expose failure of local optimization on mixed rotations; PD-11's global
Givens search solves its declared finite four-dimensional class. That is an
optimization result in a synthetic class, not identifiable language-model
reasoning components. NAN-BPD groups near-degenerate singular components and
tests equivalent GL reparameterizations before interpreting hard masks.

## Readout closure and strong competing controls

The earlier [two-case NF4/BF16 pilot](https://github.com/MoralityLabAI/LoopedTransformers/blob/23248448c177f511cdc70327a96aad4406d137b8/papers/shared_weights_dark_fibers/pilot_nf4_bf16_20261117/README.md)
has a clean task floor on both matched development cases. Its runtime and
memory figures used a resident output head and a different arm/depth schedule.
NAN-BPD changes the instrument prospectively: CPU full-vocabulary head, native
chat-format control and new source-discriminating pairs. It does not pool the
new data with those historical cases or rewrite their interpretation.

[Dark Fibers v015](https://github.com/MoralityLabAI/LoopedTransformers/pull/161)
retains the BPD residual-cancellation, density and identity-control failures.
It separates an unmeasured outcome-ensemble floor from a single-reference KL.
The [online SVD comparison](https://github.com/MoralityLabAI/LoopedTransformers/pull/160)
reports its registered CPU non-inferiority criterion passing on two TRM
checkpoints, while online superiority remains unsupported. It also records the
absent trained CI network and unresolved original CUDA identity control.
These findings require source-locked online SVD, full residuals and actual
checkpoint state closure as controls; they do not certify a trained BPD gate.

[ROC-05B](https://github.com/MoralityLabAI/LoopedTransformers/pull/163) fails
its primary task-gradient steering contrast against a physically matched
input-level control. [EIM-01](https://github.com/MoralityLabAI/EvalMath/pull/35)
separates current and next readouts, source-conditioned geometry and causal
effect matrices. Its input augmentation ties the synthetic BPD performance.
NAN-BPD consequently requires source-input counterfactuals and distinct pair
targets, rather than interpreting hidden displacement as verified reasoning.

[LS-SAE12](https://github.com/MoralityLabAI/LoopedTransformers/pull/164)
preserves its registered FAIL: 8/12 positive states in each family against a
9/12 requirement, despite positive bootstrap lower bounds. Its platform
reproductions show unstable direction contrasts on unsolved states. This
motivates retaining all source worlds, numerical fixtures, raw paired outcomes,
and failed event criteria. No retrospective threshold is changed here.

The existing LOOPSCOPE-04 gym's sampled delayed observability Gramian measures
a local finite-horizon response in sampled directions; it is not a full-kernel
certificate. Its manifold-preserving swaps and matched dose motivate the
sequence-context fiber used here. A recent experiment also named LOOPSCOPE-04
([PR169](https://github.com/MoralityLabAI/LoopedTransformers/pull/169)) studies
supervised activation-gradient alignment in a separate small TRM. Its prospective
architecture/task registration is not a Nanbeige result and is not conflated
with the older causal quotient gym.

## Compactification controls

At the pinned Compactification-Theory main head, SCM-20 and SCM-21 fail their
native causal-mediation criteria. SCM-22's norm-matched synthetic CPU comparison
favors unrestricted PCA, with a head-row-space versus kernel confound. SCM-23
fails the primary fiber-fidelity threshold in two of three seeds and retains
its calibration/holdout overlap and cross-machine discrepancies. These results
motivate exact current-readout parity, clean loss, random-dark controls and an
explicit separation between continuous observability and task mediation.

## Statistical scope

The [EvalMath power skill](https://github.com/MoralityLabAI/EvalMath/blob/accf4d9040bee8daeabc6b07003bb484708996c0/skills/evalmath-mechinterp-power/SKILL.md)
requires independent-root sham calibration and an untouched confirmation readout.
Its hard stop includes: “Fewer than the planned independent roots” and “No sealed
held-out readout”. Four development roots cannot satisfy either confirmation
requirement, or calibrate a 95th-percentile sham bound with 95% confidence.
The strict pre-data refusal is archived instead of filling in invented shams.

[FSI-01](https://github.com/MoralityLabAI/EvalMath/pull/44) supplies exact paired
accounting, cluster uncertainty and multiplicity correction. Rootwise sign-flip
calculations here are conditional sensitivity analyses: the algorithm labels
were not randomly exchanged, and sign symmetry is unestablished. Development
outcomes cannot be promoted to confirmation by a small nominal p-value.

EvalMath's [ROC-01 interface](https://github.com/MoralityLabAI/EvalMath/blob/accf4d9040bee8daeabc6b07003bb484708996c0/research/observer_calculus/roc01_evaluator.py)
requires fixed checkpoint, observer and clock across measured probes, plus
norm-matched random controls and complete intervention arms before declaring
finite hypothesis separation. Its idealized categorical tables are tutorial
fixtures. NAN-BPD's checkpoint and readout clocks are similarly frozen, while
an incomplete BPD arm family cannot earn a causal-confirmation flag.

The layer-21/channel-2279 observation comes from a
[community discussion](https://huggingface.co/Nanbeige/Nanbeige4.2-3B/discussions/20)
using MLX quantization and a few prompts. This campaign independently measures
raw and post-normalization values in the pinned BF16 and bitsandbytes NF4
backends. A repeatable loop marker alone does not demonstrate source-specific
reasoning information.
