preserved in the append-only queue and independent-report logs. No model retry.

dependencies unchanged. The six-cell model queue continues in its original
order. Change no checkpoint, fitting step, source roster, gate, cached state,
SAE dictionary, control, formula, grouping, threshold or scientific endpoint.

literal scope-only substitutions, and executes it in a fresh private module

registration and implementation before the first amended real-cache analysis.

cached summaries and retains all its original source/checkpoint/audit checks.

append-only closed-stage events. It waits for an original numerical reporter

training window, and defers any remaining work until the model queue exits.

original queue can publish repaired closed summaries at its next milestone.

runs the unchanged original reporter, publishes only its two aggregate files,
