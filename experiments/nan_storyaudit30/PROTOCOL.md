# Early bounded audit of every RAM28 native story trajectory

This is a CPU-only observation/audit registration, not a new inference
condition, new prompt, new competence gate or replacement for the full gym.
The RAM28 source order already places all 72 native story trajectories first:
12 source roots (3 CAL/9 DEV), 24 counterfactual worlds, main/copy/erased arms,
actual/first-order/nested recorded beliefs, all 32/64/96-event strata. Original
story sources, parsers, endpoints and RAM28 kernels were frozen before inference.
This separate early audit is frozen after initial RAM28 execution began but
before inspecting or grading its generated text/states. Execution metadata and
earlier studies are exposed. No output-based selection or formal inference.

Consume every complete `closed.jsonl` entry bound to those 72 fixed source slots,
once the whole story block closes. If the GPU worker ends earlier, wait for its
actual supervisor/serialized host to end before auditing the available closed
story prefix; missing worlds stay unknown with explicit whole-roster bounds.
Unclosed artifacts never become grades. The original source oracle and bounded
512-character parser supply unchanged final/checkpoint/path/joint/format
endpoints; a correct final answer alone is not a joint trace success.

Recheck source oracles, exact pinned prompt IDs, generated-token decoding and
stopping order, per-prefix histories, every closed-record/state hash, all 40
uint16 BF16 vectors per prefix, physical widths and finite values, native
local-write identity, boundary/native-output shapes, and paired batch/padding
bindings. Saved top-score/normalizer checks do not reconstruct unsaved logits
or independently certify argmax. No model replay. Keep raw replies, IDs, states
and individual grades local; publish every aggregate stratum and endpoint.

The selected block is native baseline only. Full RAM28 1,549 baseline / 216
actual A/B-pair scope stays intact, with substantial SAE/BPD analysis serialized
after GPU exit. This early audit performs no SAE encoding, factor/controller
analysis, fit or intervention and makes no cross-precision comparison. Further
BF16 remains deferred under the user’s host-RAM policy. Final parameter
verification belongs to the later canonical receipt, not this live early audit.

One model-free CPU audit, no Torch/CUDA, one numerical thread, Below Normal;
admit only with at least 5 GiB system available RAM, reserve 4 GiB and cap audit
private memory at 0.75 GiB, sampled by a two-second self-only watchdog. If the
model ends during an overlapping audit, stop only this audit to yield the lane
to the canonical host; preserve the failure without automatic retry. The tiny
stdlib follower holds authenticated actual model/supervisor handles, uses only
append-only journals, and never opens live atomic progress. It never terminates
or changes model, math or unrelated processes. It publishes a successful early
audit once; no model/CPU-analysis replay or loop of duplicate milestones.

Roots are the units; worlds, arms, prefixes, checkpoint/path steps are repeated.
Missingness can follow deterministic order/resource stops. Output traces are
not proof of faithful internal reasoning. Causal/sham floors and power remain
uncalibrated; zero formal comparisons, `REDESIGN_OR_PILOT_ONLY`, no donor
transfer or reopening previous failed gates. The original 168-case test stays
sealed. Finishing this audit does not complete or pause the continuous goal.
