# RAM-12 technical report | Native Lean state traces and bounded proof-action scoring

**Report date:** 15 November 2026  
**Status:** Historical proof evidence certified; registered retrospective CPU criterion failed; fresh theorem-family transfer pending  
**Repositories:** [MathCan](https://github.com/patrickdugan/MathCan) and [LoopedTransformers](https://github.com/MoralityLabAI/LoopedTransformers)

## Executive finding

RAM-12 closes an evidence gap in the RAM-11F curriculum by capturing genuine Lean goal states immediately before every action in 76 previously certified complete proof scripts. The public MathCan runner completed **152/152 full-source Lean 4.35 compilations**. The resulting archive contains 282 action-position observations, reduced to **230 unique original theorem/prefix/next-action positives** over 176 prefix contexts. Comparing with the independently replayed RAM-08 original action corpus identifies **138 exact overlaps and 92 additional state-conditioned positive actions**.

A separately frozen 150-update CPU residual-head experiment used these labels with the original RAM-08 trunks held fixed. On 19 eligible historical holdback contexts, Looped64 improved first-ranked certified positives **4 to 7**, while Looped32 remained **6 to 6** and Flat64 **6 to 6**. The registered descriptive target required two of three improvements and **failed**. All observations are retrospective: these roots originated in the original RAM-08 training corpus, and only known successful or compiler-rejected candidate actions were scored. The result is evidence of a better action ranking for one historical recurrent model, not a new theorem-level capability result.

## 2. Native state extraction and trust boundary

A read-only `ram12_observe` Lean tactic was inserted before each tactic in an already certified, complete original MathCan theorem script. The target's original proof was replaced with exactly the proposed original actions. Two separate full-source Lean 4.35 compilations had to succeed, with agreeing printed pending goals, visible definitions, script order, output markers, and allowed axiom inventory. The original transitive axiom allowlist is `propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound` (or no axioms).

| Native measurement | Verified count |
|---|---:|
| Complete existing MathCan theorem scripts | 76 |
| Distinct original theorem roots | 22 |
| Independent full-source compilations | **152** |
| Action-position observations | 282 |
| Deduplicated exact-prefix goal contexts | 176 |
| Deduplicated positive state/action labels | **230** |
| Exact matches to original RAM-08 effects | 138 |
| Newly observed positive state/action pairs | **92** |

Each positive action has an explicit suffix that closes its original theorem in a fully compiled script. Alternatives without such a witness remain **censored**. The 92 additional examples now have native Lean goal states, while the original RAM-11F corpus had only source-certified script-prefix labels.

Independent artifact checks confirmed the full worklist SHA, native archive SHA, label SHA, paired source/log hashes, and all 76 proof cases. An additional local checksum pass over the **17 files** in the bundled RAM-12 source/results/weights archive found no mismatch against its SHA-256 manifest. The ZIP serves as a reproducibility package, not as a substitute for native Lean verification.

## 3. Historical split and supervised action objective

The 22 original training theorems were assigned by the fixed hash `SHA256("RAM12-FRESH-STATE-20261115/" + file + ":" + theorem) mod 6`:

| Cohort | Theorem roots | Prefix states | Positive actions | Contexts with known positive and negative |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Fit | 14 | 105 | 137 | 51 |
| Development | 3 | 24 | 31 | 14 |
| Historical holdback | 5 | 47 | 62 | **19** |

Among 137 fit positives, **83** have original RAM-08 compiler failure-or-unchanged alternatives at the identical theorem and exact tactic prefix. The other **54** are positive-only and contribute no invented failure label. Training assembled **778 positive-versus-failure pairs** and **20 shorter-versus-longer pairs where both choices had complete certified proof suffixes**.

The frozen original policies have approximately 85k parameters (Looped32), 207k (Looped64), and 182k (Flat64). All three original trunks and checkpoints remain byte-identical. A separate small residual head modifies the original tactic score:

`r(s,a) = r0(s,a) + 0.35 * tanh(w^T z0(s,a) + b)`.

Training used 150 deterministic CPU AdamW steps, batch 32, learning rate 0.003, weight decay 0.01, a 0.02 residual regularizer, and a 0.2 preference weight for shorter observed certified suffixes. The residual score change is bounded by 0.35. Consequently, any original top action that leads its rivals by strictly more than **0.70** retains first place for every possible residual-head weight. This is a ranking bound; it is not a theorem certificate.

## 4. Registered CPU results

Each score below selects only among source-certified positive actions and original compiler failure-or-unchanged candidates at the **same exact tactic prefix**. The historical holdback offers 19 eligible contexts and 688 evaluated positive/negative candidate pairs. Pair observations share states, and should not be treated as 688 independent samples.

| Policy | Holdback top-1 baseline | After fit | Positive-over-failed pairs before | After |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Looped32, seed 29 | 6/19 | 6/19 | 594/688 (86.3%) | 624/688 (90.7%) |
| **Looped64, seed 43** | **4/19** | **7/19** | 492/688 (71.5%) | 531/688 (77.2%) |
| Flat64, seed 29 | 6/19 | 6/19 | 531/688 (77.2%) | 548/688 (79.7%) |

The registered target required **at least two of three** models to improve holdback top-1 selection, with no model losing more than one context. It **failed**: only Looped64 improved. The 3/19 gain occurred on a small, previously exposed historical cohort; its interpretation is exploratory. All three models improved pairwise ranking on these known candidate sets, which does not measure full theorem search.

The auxiliary shortest-*observed*-certified-suffix selection was unchanged in the holdback across all models (Looped32 1/2, Looped64 2/2, Flat64 1/2). Observed suffix length is an upper bound on the shortest possible proof, not a proof of global optimality.

## 5. Posthoc strict-rejection sensitivity

After the registered first fit, a review found that **6 of 1,652** original label-zero candidate rows were actually accepted by Lean but left the visible goal state unchanged. The first-run receipt remains immutable. The separate posthoc analysis used only candidate actions with `after.accepted=false`: **1,646 strict compiler rejections**, yielding 772 training positive/negative pairs.

Top-1 holdback selection remained **6→6, 4→7, 6→6**. Pairwise after-fit wins were Looped32 **624/688**, Looped64 **530/688**, Flat64 **549/688**. These comparisons retain the historical selected-candidate interpretation and do not satisfy the original descriptive criterion.

## 6. Limits and next registered experiment

The 22 roots were members of the historical original RAM-08 TRAIN cohort. The newer hash split therefore cannot create truly independent generalization evidence for models originally exposed to those roots. The recorded 19-state holdback is small. A top-ranked certified action within a selected pool has not been shown to produce a full proof when embedded in a fresh policy-controlled search.

The registered RAM-08 PEF theorem comparison remains **0/120 search cases scored**, and RAM-10's fresh new-source theorem evaluation remains **unscored**. Historical 76/76 certified proofs are for existing TRAIN theorems and must not be counted toward either prospective benchmark.

Next, gather compiler-checked counterfactual tactic outcomes at the 92 newly captured prefix-action contexts using the unchanged grammar. Freeze a **new theorem-family-disjoint prospective split before source access**, then run entire native Lean proof searches against recurrent, flat, native-order, global-frequency, and shape-frequency controls. Every accepted theorem requires complete source recompilation and a separate axiom-gated replay. Preserve source SHA, model checkpoints, action budgets, resource receipts, and every failed proof attempt.
