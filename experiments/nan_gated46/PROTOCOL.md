Register eight new focused CAL pointer roots, both counterfactual worlds,
literal-copy and graph-erased controls. A separately executed source oracle
requires at least six both-correct focused roots, eight both-correct copy
roots and exact erased twin identity before admitting edits. It returns only
root-level correctness bits and the aggregate gate to the model worker.
For a native-correct focused twin pair, decode the two native unrestricted
pass-2 tokens and bind their parsed labels to the pinned bare-label rows.
Use these native decisions to orient the edit and endpoint.

Measure the 0.25% phase-1 last-token residual displacement in DELAY39's
seven fixed IO18 writes. Project the donor coefficient difference into the
continuous bare-label margin null. Require donor cosine at least 0.8.
The matched span sham is seeded independently of outcomes and orthogonal
to both the margin gradient and the margin-null donor in the same write
span. Include positive and negative signs. All four realized edits must
match the requested radius within 3%, direction cosine at least 0.98, and
pairwise reached-dose differences within 3% of radius.

Before any edited second pass for a root-world, require all four reached
BF16 phase-1 heads to preserve the clean unrestricted winner, the
bare-label margin within 0.125 logits, and every vocabulary logit within
0.125. During each actual B2 edit, match the complete planned phase-1
logits at the physical pass boundary before entering pass 2. A boundary
mismatch aborts the trajectory before the next decoder call. Preserve
the clean companion, self closure, reached read coefficients and exact
physical positions. All unsupported rows remain in the roster with their
reason; a failed arm yields zero admitted edited continuations for that
world.

The primary descriptive endpoint is the signed sham-adjusted phase-2
donor-versus-recipient margin response, with each signed slope normalized
by the sum of the realized doses and rescaled to the nominal dose. The
prospective instrument threshold is 0.125 logits. Also retain the
independently judged recipient and donor first-token outcomes, read
coefficients, complete signed contrast count, and every unscored world.
Eight already registered calibration roots are insufficient to establish
a sham tail or population effect. The original sealed ARG-03 split remains
outside this pilot.

local DELAY39 queue, the predecessor inventory and the shared GPU mutex.
Use the pinned NF4 then BF16 interpreter and target, one model at a time,
and the unchanged local host and GPU resource limits. The CPU
