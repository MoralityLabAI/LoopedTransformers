# LRF-02: generator–critic learning from independently checked proof rewards

LRF-01 passed (run 45380937893, 61/61 complete historical model traces, 80/80 deceptive complete controls rejected, zero false-positive reward). This new prospective CPU experiment trains a small proof scheduler and a separate critic. The native LRF-01 gate adjudicates submitted proof candidates. The grammar is restricted Horn propositions and integer traces; no arbitrary executable Lean is accepted.

## Data freeze

Reuse LR-01's public Horn generator and independent structured renderer. Rebuild the historical corpus only for disjoint structural fingerprint checking against all 1280 frozen historical roots (verify frozen task/source hashes); never train on its labels or traces. Fresh seed 120201: 64 train standard graphs, 16 calibration standard graphs, 24 standard test and 24 deep test graphs; all whole-graph WL fingerprints unique across both historical and prospective corpora. Public generated holdout, with subproof motifs potentially recurring. Each task's intended type, permitted foundation and proof action budget are verifier-owned. No test-based checkpoint selection. Use CPU Torch 2.5.1 and Lean 4.19.0.

## Generator and critic

Generator: 16-hidden-unit MLP scoring legal novel Horn rule firings with public structural features (goal reachability/distance, known set, rule incidence, remaining step budget). Train two seeds 171/173, 10 epochs, AdamW .003, batch64, action-set imitation of the LR-01 unique-producer dependency oracle on train only. Teacher trajectories include 15% random legal exploration, fixed max 10 steps, stop when goal reached or no action. Generator produces actual greedy and seeded stochastic traces; never append missing oracle actions. Critic: 32-hidden-unit MLP over public candidate structural features (statement sizes and atom masks, corresponding intended-claim and candidate facts/goals/rule summaries, trace progress and mode), without native reward/axiom receipts or a precomputed contract equality/validity bit. Train only on completed native train receipts, binary cross entropy, 10 epochs AdamW .003 batch64. Unknown receipts are censored, not negative. This is learned ranking of a small structured candidate pool, not free-form critique generation.

Shared pool per task: three generator rollouts (one greedy, two sampled, fixed seeds derived from case ID and fit seed), one greedy easier-target trace, one added-goal-hypothesis trace, one extra-direct-rule trace, fixed unauthorized axiom and alias fixtures, one correct-claim rollout with an unused unauthorized axiom. The two axiom proof modes are renderer-controlled adversarial fixtures, never permissions granted to an external model. Exact native reward checks every distinct candidate once per task/seed/stage; record duplicate proposals. Train generation and critique in two stages: initial imitation; native adjudication of train pools; jointly update generator and critic for ten fixed epochs using native-certified original-claim traces (additional generator action-set supervision from actual successful traces, plus frozen imitation groups to avoid drift) and native critic labels. The generator need not be differentiable through Lean. This is alternating supervised feedback from verified proofs, not policy-gradient RL.

## Evaluation

Generate fresh calibration and test candidate pools using each frozen final generator. Replay every distinct candidate independently with the same native verifier, archiving actual calls and source hashes. Compare first proposal, generator-first order, seeded random, learned critic order, and exact structural screening (known task claim, allowed renderer mode and complete legal trace), all over the identical pool for each seed. The latter is a strong deterministic control for this grammar and should approach the pool ceiling; no claim of superiority over exact checking. Report counterfactual 1/2/4/8-candidate budget coverage from cached native receipts, actual total native calls, complete deceptive proposals, unknown/censored attempts, false approvals before native gating, zero false accepted rewards, pool feasibility, initial/final generator-only success, parameter counts and per-split receipts. Each rank score is not a proof. Primary gate: both seeds' learned critic retains at least 90% of attainable native successes within two proposals on each test stratum, and at most 5% of completed deceptive candidates score above its 0.5 approval threshold. Secondary comparison with the exact filter and initial generator. Preserve failures. No historical scores changed; no RAM-18 test sources opened.
