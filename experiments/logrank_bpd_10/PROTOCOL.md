Even nonzero parity masks only (no singleton labels or side information). k in {16,32}, incidence rank r in {8,12}, six fresh worlds per (k,r), root IDs 3100+100*k+10*r+s for s=0..5. Training: r independent basis rows followed by 3r random nonzero span rows, repetitions allowed. 32 new in-span queries, 32 nonzero even out-of-span queries, 48 distinct unused in-span probe candidates. Noise independent binary symmetric flips at 0,.15,.35, rate known to Bayesian decoder. Compare basis solve ignoring redundancy, exact rowspace posterior, hard-sign straight-through optimizer (four restarts, 200 full-batch Adam steps, lr .05; pick min observed training error), exact posterior threshold .9 abstention. Four extra noisy calls: entropy-selected vs random unused probe, same budget and common independent noise draws by step. No direct test queries. Uniform hidden signs, no target leakage. Score separate in/out span, calibration/coverage, exact posterior confidence, paired effects across roots; no significance/confirmation claim. Hard-sign optimizer is a BPD-inspired proxy, not native TRM/BPD. Additional verifier probes restricted to existing span, so cannot remove out-of-span ambiguity. Record all root outcomes, query masks and observations for independent replay. No protected data.
