# LOGRANK-BPD-12 development protocol
Initial ranks r=5,8; total width d=r+3. Seeds 120000+100*r+i; i=0..7 training, i=100..111 held-out development. Uniform hidden bits, known quotient coordinates. 4r initial observations: basis plus random nonzero in-span parity masks. Target masks excluded from all observations. Eight old and 24 new target masks (eight per independent new direction), total old target weight .25 or .75 by seed parity. Candidate pool: 16 old, 24 single-new-direction, 8 multi-new-direction masks. A candidate may repeat an initial observation but cannot be acquired twice. Noise .15,.35 known, common independent noise uniforms by call step. 12 extra calls. Primary weighted Brier score; secondary old/new accuracy, .9 confidence coverage, acquired rank, runtime. Seven policies: random; old-only exact-gain; rank-first exact-gain; entropy; exact weighted one-step Brier; factorized BP gain; learned ridge imitation using BP and geometry features. Train ridge on separate exact-gain and random trajectories, standardized features, regularization 10. BP uses 40 damped iterations and span membership by GF(2) elimination. Only oracle policies use exact posterior for selection; all policies use exact posterior for scoring. No held-out tuning, native intervention, protected data or confirmation claim. Independent unit is world. All outcomes retained.
