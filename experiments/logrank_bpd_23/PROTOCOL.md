# LOGRANK-BPD-23 development protocol
Fresh 12/96D worlds, three roots per width/family (chain, two-hub chain, global triples), seeds 230000+1000*n+100*family+i via 22 generator. Same 2x2 popularity/locality workloads as 22, four 16-query epochs and repeated unary evidence updates, paired .15/.35 noise. Refuse max bag scope >6. Compare direct, LRU, planned cold, warm marginal, bounded local and gated local at 16/64 KiB (11 arms). Local starts from warm marginal at 16 KiB or planned cold at 64 KiB, a fixed routing decision based on 21/22. At most two rounds, six candidate targets per round; rank additions by current miss reduction times cold subtree cost per byte; rank victims by analogous estimated lost saving per byte, evict as many low-density victims as needed. Score exact current unsigned lazy-fill work from valid residents; accept strictly improving best candidate, deterministic lexicographic tie. Gated variant skips a round when current unsigned work <= (number of candidates+1)*edge count, and accepts only gain greater than that same charge. The charge is a deterministic planning proxy, not FLOPs or elapsed time; include all planning, shortlist construction, lookup and execution in measured sequential runtime. Fixed byte reservations, same logical cap as 21/22. No future evidence or demand used. Primary 96D hub 16/64 KiB vs corresponding routed baseline; all factorial regimes retained, three independent roots per stratum, descriptive only. Rotate arm order; no concurrent timing runs. Independent exact posterior reference each epoch, replay demand, shortlist/target decisions, actual predicted work, cache ledger and budgets. Preserve dense refusals, all failures, and root records. No native training, confirmation or protected data.
