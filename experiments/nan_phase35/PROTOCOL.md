# NAN-PHASE-35 read-only phase geometry

The already identified layer-21 residual coordinate 2279 is the primary
candidate. Its causal status remains open. This audit asks how much of the
observed NF4 phase-1 versus phase-2 geometry it accounts for, whether a
CAL-fitted scalar threshold transfers to the DEV stories, and how its
contribution to matched A/B differences compares with its contribution to
within-world phase changes.

The inputs are the 72 immutable, independently audited STORYAUDIT30 NF4
episodes, with all 3,990 prefixes. Scope includes 3 CAL and 9 DEV roots,
both worlds, and the main, copy and erased arms. Recheck the frozen
aggregate, selection, episode, prefix, and archive hashes before use.
Open only closed records named by the old audit. Decode physical BF16
residual vectors at layers 17 and 21, phases 1 and 2. Keep all registered
prefixes and report a separate first-prefix view before generated text
can influence comparisons. The A/B comparison uses that prefix in both
worlds, at each phase separately. Record how many pairs differ in semantic
length or execution shape.

For each pair of vectors, report full cosine, leave-one-coordinate-out
cosine shift, and the coordinate's fraction of squared Euclidean
difference. For phase-paired vectors also report per-phase energy share.
Signed split directions and unweighted midpoint thresholds are fitted
using root-macro first-prefix CAL means, pooling CAL arms. Apply the
frozen thresholds to both phases of every retained prefix. Report balanced
accuracy by episode and then root, with separate first-prefix results.
Use 2278, 2280, eight seeded random residual coordinates, and layer 17
as descriptive comparison controls. Report all registered coordinates.
The primary view is layer 21 on DEV main, with first-prefix and all-prefix
outputs carried together. Records with degenerate cosine denominators are
explicitly counted and only affect that metric's denominator.

Evidence units are source roots, with repeated worlds, arms, prefixes,
layers, and coordinates. This is a retrospective diagnostic on exposed
development tasks and one checkpoint. Coordinate omission changes an
offline metric. A live causal intervention, matched physical-dose shams,
behavioral competence, sham floor, and fresh confirmation are separate
requirements. No power estimate or formal comparison is justified.
The original 168-case test remains sealed. No model/Torch, fit, GPU work,
SAE projection, grading, or reopening of live atomic records occurs.

One CPU thread at Below Normal, 5 GiB available before admission, 4 GiB
reserve, 0.75 GiB private cap, and 600 seconds. The watchdog can stop
only its own process. All per-record observations stay local.
