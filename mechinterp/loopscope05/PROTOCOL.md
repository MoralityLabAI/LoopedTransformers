## Gradient geometry

For every heldout sample, at k=0..3 compute h[k], native proposed state update v[k]=F(h[k])-h[k], and local current-head supervised gradient g_local[k]=(softmax(H h[k])-onehot(target)) W_head. Calculate true future gradient g_future[k] = d/dh[k] SUM over all 81 final token CEs after exactly (4-k) native shared F blocks. This includes cross-token influences and uses ground-truth test labels ONLY for evaluation, never for feature selection or SAE fitting. Compute full autograd on the current frozen checkpoint, with the 81-token CE SUM so that local and future derivatives have matched scaling. At each k, log the tokenwise cosine(v,-g_local), cosine(v,-g_future), their difference, gradient-to-gradient cosine, gradient norm, first-order predicted decrease -g_future dot v, and actual difference between current-head token CE and the next-head CE. Preserve raw per-sequence averages over 81 tokens.

For each of three primary seeds, report mean cosine with the future gradient and 95% bootstrap percentile intervals from 256 full-sequence resamples (2000 deterministic draws, root SHA256 of LGA02|42044|seed|k|future). The preregistered primary POSITIVE classification requires that at least 2/4 depth-specific future-gradient cosine lower bounds exceed zero and original LGA-01 checkpoint reproduction pass, in at least 2/3 final-only seeds. Report all depth contrasts with local cosine and both outcomes. The signed local-vs-future difference has no predetermined direction.

At k=1,2, project v into FP64 measured rowspace of the 10x48 head and exact orthogonal head-nullspace. Train-only SAE decoder active span for each test token is the span of four decoder columns chosen by input-only TopK4 codes. Project native v into this span and its orthogonal complement using rank-4 QR/SVD or stable solve, with rank admission above relative singular value 1e-8 and both projected norms above 1e-8. Record per-sequence mean active-decoder and head-visible squared update energy, retained Top4 support between consecutive loops, future gradient alignment with both physical components, and head-null future gradient norm. Compare native fourth-pass full 81-token mean CE, target-goal CE and correct-digit vs strongest CLEAN rival. The first is the causal endpoint corresponding to global future gradient. Preserve original 10-output logits at selected goal and clean/zero-edit exact parity. If active projection, its orthogonal complement or head-null is degenerate, mark case ineligible for paired comparison and preserve source goal without resampling or choosing a different intervention.

For each admitted case and arm, compare measured final sequence-mean CE change against the first-order predicted gradient change g_future dot edit divided by 81; save both. Predeclared paired contrasts: SAE-active versus SAE-orthogonal, and head-null versus equal-norm random. A causal contrast is labeled POSITIVE/NEGATIVE by sign only if its interval excludes zero; all other conclusions INCONCLUSIVE. No winner chosen by test effects.

## Scientific boundaries

Positive future-gradient alignment does not prove the recurrent block implements SGD, and active dictionary projections do not establish intrinsic semantic meaning. SAEs can be non-identifiable, and the head-null component may influence downstream output through later shared loops. The study is an algorithmic cyclic-copy control on 44K recurrent weights rather than a 7M Sudoku TRM or Nanbeige LLM. Deep_020 on one preselected seed is a descriptive objective comparison. Original LOOPSCOPE-04 POSITIVE and SMC-27 FAIL remain historically fixed.
