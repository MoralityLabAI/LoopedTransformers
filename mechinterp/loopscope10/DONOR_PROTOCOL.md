## Why a donor endpoint is needed

On exposed LGA-06 IID sources the original frozen teachers reached **98.62%** correct-token accuracy after their trained four passes. A new experiment with 64 strings and eight fixed goal tokens yields 512 goal measurements per teacher and only about **7** expected naturally wrong goals per teacher (512*(1-.9862)). These are nested measurements with just **three previously exposed teachers**, precluding a well-powered wrong-to-right correction test. LGA-06 Block9 at 56.60% original-four-pass accuracy is also poor native competence for mechanism identification. The twelve-pass baseline falls to approximately chance accuracy. The original LGA-07 proposal stays registered and its source 31059 **unopened**; this separate donor protocol uses its own seed **31060**.

### Frozen physical arm

At actual native depth **k=2** (primary), compute full recipient and donor states from their naturally reached native F applications, with the original same F/weights. Define the donor difference at the goal as `u=h_donor[k,j]-h_recipient[k,j]`. Construct the recipient's physical energy/head tangent space `K=ker([W_head; dE_shared(h_recipient)/dh_j])` by the previously merged float64 SVD instrument, relative cutoff **1e-9**, absolute **1e-12**. The selected additive edit is `P_K u`, normalized to fixed physical radius **rho=0.25*||F(h_recipient)[j]-h_recipient[j]||**. A zero or nearly zero `P_K u` is ineligible, counted in intention-to-treat, never replaced or reselected.

Generate exactly **eight** SHA-seeded isotropic K shams with the same physical L2 radius, from recipient state only and no donor labels. Also record a fixed-sign `-selected` control, a same-radius unprojected `u` donor-direction control, and whole-token donor state replacement with its **natural larger dose** as an explicit positive/oracle control. The last control must never be used in an equal-dose mechanism comparison.

All source plans (K, selected vector, shams, hashes, sign, dose) are frozen before final truth-label scoring. Readout is the full original recipient model continued for exactly **2** further native tied-block applications to the original four-pass head. The ten goal logits, all final 81 predicted classes, finite current-head displacement, finite shared-potential displacement, actual hidden edit dose and later energy changes are mandatory raw receipts.

## Endpoints and controls

- Primary development comparison: donor-target **correct-cell rate** from the projected donor direction minus the median of eight same-K equal-dose random controls. Every 64 independent base IDs is a cluster of eight goal cells, with three old frozen teacher seeds as separate model units. Preserve intent-to-treat all 512 cells per seed, plus a separate *diagnostic* eligible-population result. Goal eligibility is clean recipient AND clean legal donor correct at the original four-pass horizon; require at least 80% eligible goals per teacher for an interpretable task benchmark. Eligibility cannot change the primary ITT denominator.
- **Engineering-positive diagnostic** (NOT science confirmation) needs >=2 of 3 teachers with >=0.15 absolute ITT projected-donor minus median-sham gain and a positive 2,000-resample whole-base-ID bootstrap lower bound on those teachers; donor control must correctly yield the donor target in >=95% of eligible clean donors. Otherwise keep the first-run DEVELOPMENT_NULL_OR_INELIGIBLE verdict.
- Secondary: fixed-sign inversion, unprojected same-dose `u`, full donor-token positive control, goal margin and CE changes (correct donor label only after plans frozen), finite current-head and energy invariance violations, semantic oracle condition, and native Jacobian energy-tangent *first-order* future derivative norm. Never infer exact finite invariance from the nullspace.
- A k=6→12 extrapolated stress arm with identical donor source pairing and fixed radius is **descriptive only**. The native twelve-pass model is largely incompetent, so do not promote any successful edit to a task-mechanism result. Do not average it with k=2 for a PASS.

A physically matched sham living in the identical K is a stronger control than arbitrary isotropic noise but does not fully match the future true-target gradient. Conditional mediation claims require an additional future-gradient-matched null study. Donor source membership and the target difference are legal structural facts, rather than model-internal knowledge of a correct class.
