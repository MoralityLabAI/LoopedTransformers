## Question and frozen original model

Can an additive edit in a learned TRM's **current-head-null and frozen-energy-tangent physical fiber** transfer an independently defined *legal donor* correct digit through native future recurrent passes, better than eight matched isotropic edits in the exact same fiber?

This is a stricter question than the earlier LGA-05 observation that one external scalar E(h) decreases along the original four steps, and distinct from the LGA-06 FAIL on 12-pass transfer. Original LGA-02 44,001-parameter 4-pass model teachers 103/211/307 and original LGA-05 scalar potentials were reused with exact unchanged tensor SHA checks. The previously exposed teacher roots are developmental, not new independent root-level scientific confirmation.

## First source and experimental endpoint

After a byte-and-tensor pretest lock, 64 unique 81-digit IID base strings from seed `31060` were created. For every base and each fixed goal j in `(0,10,20,30,40,50,60,70)`, a legal donor changed ONLY input predecessor `j-1 mod 81` from digit d to `d%9+1`. The donor target is the correctly right-shifted donor input; exactly one target location changes. The donor was generated by source structure, before selecting or scoring physical edits.

At true native k=2, each teacher reached recipient and donor states independently. The selected direction was the projection of the naturally reached **single goal-token** donor difference into `ker(W_head) ∩ ker(∇_j E_shared)`, normalized to `rho=.25 ||F(h)[j]-h[j]||`. The 10-class head had rank ten and the joint head/energy constraints had rank eleven in all 1,536 original k2 cases, leaving a **37-dimensional fiber**. Every original case passed geometry admission.

Eight SHA-seeded same-fiber isotropic random shams received exactly the same physical L2 dose. Signed inversion and unprojected equal-dose donor controls were recorded. A naturally reached full-donor **TOKEN** replacement used its original larger L2 dose as a positive control. All edits continued the original tied block for two true remaining passes to the original four-pass head. At k6→12 the same experiment was performed as a predeclared **descriptive stress**.

Primary within-goal metric: `correct donor target under projected edit − median(binary correctness of eight same-K shams)`. Effects are clustered first by 64 original base IDs (eight paired donor goals each) within each teacher; 2,000 fixed-seed source-cluster bootstraps. The original engineering gate needs two of the three old teachers with paired improvement ≥+15 percentage points and bootstrap lower >0, plus ≥80% jointly competent recipient/donor clean goals and ≥95% correct donor-target outcomes under full donor-token replacement among those competent goals.

The same-fiber donor direction improves a *continuous* target probability measure relative to shams on the new source strings. The registered behavioral mechanism threshold was correct-target recovery, for which the effect was below one percentage point in all three exposed teacher roots. These CE/margin summaries are a post-result analysis of prespecified secondary metrics, with no familywise-corrected new discovery claim.

The physical restriction is severe: the 37D head/energy-nullspace retains only about **one-third of the donor-token direction's original Euclidean norm**. The registered small edit radius is about **one-seventh of the full donor-token replacement dose**. Source-conditioned donor semantic information may be spread across multiple token positions, and a single-token replacement recovers the alternative target only ~64–68% of the time even at natural larger dose. It therefore fails its own positive-control prerequisite.

An energy-tangent gradient inner product of zero is an infinitesimal condition. The measured *finite* shared-E after-edit changes for the projected arm were +0.178, +0.104 and +0.218 (mean total E units per example) across seeds 103/211/307; nonlinear curvature is present. Finite head/E preservation must be checked in any larger-radius follow-up.

## Interpretation and next discriminating experiment

The negative first run establishes that the registered **single-goal-token, small equal-dose, energy-tangent** donor patch has negligible correct-target transfer on the three frozen 44K teachers. It leaves the broader energy-tangent delayed-computation hypothesis unresolved because (1) the natural positive donor-token control has insufficient transfer, (2) physical dose was far below the natural donor change, (3) a large fraction of the donor local direction is lost to the head/energy constraints, and (4) current token-local readout silence permits future changes mediated through other tokens.

A new prospective study should define a **whole-state** naturally reached donor positive control with exact clean donor future replay, explicitly compare recipient goal-token-only and predecessor/goal-pair edits with whole-state patching, and use a global orthogonal projection onto `ker(D head_all_tokens) ∩ ker(dE)` with physical-dose-matched null directions. Only a strong natural full-state donor positive control can isolate the loss from tangent projection or the dose budget. Include a fixed natural-donor-dose and a lower-dose arm, finite head/E checks, and source-root clustered power before opening a new seed. That design is distinct from repeating 31060 with a larger chosen dose.

Connections to BPD: this is a *physical Euclidean subspace* experiment using the robust kernel from BPD-EFD-01, not learned BPD factor singleton causal recovery, rank compression or a demonstration of intrinsic internal optimization. The observed CE change is an observer-direction diagnostic. Formal delayed observability `rank(DG_future|K)` and correct-target causal mediation remain separate claims.
