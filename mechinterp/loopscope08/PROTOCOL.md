## Interpretation

Even a positive single-potential test demonstrates that an **external scalar energy function can approximate an aspect of the ORIGINAL recurrent field on unseen source states**. It cannot establish that the frozen TRM explicitly computes that energy, uses its gradient as an optimizer, or is globally integrable. Training to native updates also makes aligned gradients a target; genuine evidence requires heldout cross-depth coherence, energy monotonicity and independent native task effects. The LGA-01 local POSITIVE, LGA-02 terminal FAIL, LGA-03 value-critic FAIL and LGA-04 external Sobolev critic POSITIVE conclusions remain unchanged.
