### Key competence and engineering findings

1. **ARC interface bug + color-support vs program-diversity resolution:** `dt03_tasks.arc_demo_tokens` emits 1-indexed symbols `1..11` (`ARC_END = 11`), which triggers CUDA gather index assertions on `PureSudokuTRM.embed_tokens = nn.Embedding(11, ...)` (`0..10`) unless bijectively shifted (`t - 1`). With 3 public demos encoded, strict `worlds.arc_analogue` rises from `0%` (DT02-A) to **`99.72%`** on unseen grids of its two training rules, while failing `0.00%` on heldout `rotate_180, fill_zero` because `color 3` has zero training target support (`color3_recall = 0.0` on 59/59 roots) and two rules cannot supervise program induction. Supplying all 4 colors across `192` training lookup programs lifts **zero-shot heldout program exactness (`64` unseen rules including `fill_zero`) to `99.92%` (`99.99%` cell accuracy)** across unseen depths `8` and `12`.
2. **Closed-loop Storyworld control completion:** Training on reachable public states under `dt03_tasks.public_reachable_policy` achieved **`3,776 / 3,776` (`100.0%`)** 12-step closed-loop terminal successes across all 59 roots at `d = 6, 8, 12` (labeled strictly as a public control-world test, not hidden-cause inference).
3. **Bimodal 4×4 Sudoku grokking under fixed 480-step single-shot budget:** Canonical class partitioning (`build_root_sudoku_splits`) guaranteed `cross_split_isomorphic_items == 0` on all 59 roots. Under a fixed 480-step budget with zero restarts, `19/59` roots grokked iterative constraint propagation (`66.45%` exact at `d=2` → `89.80%` at `d=4` → `94.49%` at `d=6` → **`95.81%` at unseen `d=8, 12`**), `16/59` reached partial accuracy (`10%–84%`), and `23/59` remained ungrokked (`< 10%`).
4. **36-pass float32 `GL(C)` gauge tolerance:** On `3/59` roots (`2604008`, `2604026`, `2604050` — all high-competence Sudoku models), `V' = V G, U' = G^{-1} U` preserved the physical operator to **`3.82e-8..3.84e-8`** relative Frobenius error, while 36 sequential recurrent invocations (`d = 12 × 3`) accumulated float32 non-associativity logit diffs of `7.71e-5..1.78e-4` (above the `5e-5` 4-pass `NC13` threshold, with zero prediction flips). Those three flags were preserved unedited in [DT04_ORIGINAL_AUDIT.json](results/DT04_ORIGINAL_AUDIT.json).

### EvalMath 59-root sham calibration and mechanistic interpretation

- **59-root nonparametric 95/95 sham calibration ([POSTLOCK_59ROOT_AND_COMPETENT_DISSECTION.json](results/POSTLOCK_59ROOT_AND_COMPETENT_DISSECTION.json)):** Across all `59` roots (`472/472` certified shams), the worst within-root signed-Jacobian sham deviation at unseen `d = 8` (`order_statistic_rank = 59`, covering the population 95th percentile at `>= 95%` confidence) is **`0.046875` (`3/64` exact boards)**, well below the registered `0.10` mechanistic floor. However, the observed mean `task_gramian_r8 - signed_sham_mean8` effect is **`+0.000265` (`SD = 0.0118`)** across all 59 roots and **`+0.000000`** on the 19 competent roots, so `EvalMath` returns **`REDESIGN_OR_PILOT_ONLY` (`pilot_effect_not_above_mechanistic_variation_floor`)**.
- **Why static state subspaces fail while `BPDModel` operator decompositions extrapolate:**
  - Forward-mode directional derivatives (`torch.func.jvp`) show a mean **one-step subspace closure defect** $\|(I - \Pi_E) DF(S) \Pi_E\|_F / \|DF(S) \Pi_E\|_F = \mathbf{0.9904}$ (`0.5657` escaping $\ker(DP)$ in one step), with mean principal angles between $E_{\text{fit}(2,4,6)}$ and depth-$k$ oracle task subspaces drifting to **`48.69°` (`d=6`), `51.77°` (`d=8`), and `53.65°` (`d=12`)**. Any static rank-8 state edit therefore acts purely through its immediate first-order task tangent, which `signed_task_matched_sham` reproduces identically.
  - In contrast, **closed-loop 50%-sparse `BPDModel` (`12/24` components per shared `o_proj`/`down_proj`, `delta_mask = 0`)** fitted solely on depths `(2, 4, 6)` achieves **`93.09%` (`d=6`), `94.90%` (`d=8`), and `95.39%` (`d=12`) exact boards** (`KL = 0.00382 → 0.00309 → 0.00286` nats) on the 19 competent roots because shared weight factors `(V, U)` act in operator space at every recurrent pass rather than assuming a depth-invariant hidden-state subspace.
