## Source anchor batch sensitivity

The frozen first ACT step extracted from a two-puzzle source batch differed from the identical puzzle extracted alone, although `x_emb` was byte-identical.

| Source | max absolute ACT1 zH difference | max absolute ACT1 zL difference |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| `test_52[0]` | 5.9604644775390625e-6 | 8.8214874267578125e-6 |
| `test_54[3]` | 6.735324859619141e-6 | 1.1499971151351929e-5 |

These small ACT1 differences were sufficient to change a fresh ACT6 signed logit by as much as 0.175644 on the 54-hole puzzle. The original historical output and fresh same-process outputs are different despite correct checkpoint/source SHA. The **exact original LS-SAE08 canonical evaluation function**, when executed twice within one process on the same source anchor, produced **bitwise-identical raw 81×9 logits** at all arms/horizons. Additional hidden-state instrumentation also produced identical logits on that anchor. This strongly disfavors the LS-SAE09 summary-measurement code itself as the source of the *same-process* variation.

The diagnostic has *not* isolated the precise cause of **cross-process** historical-versus-fresh differences. CPU execution scheduling, numerical backend path, stage-one source extraction and nonassociative recurrent amplification remain candidates. The source batch-size counterfactual demonstrates sensitivity, but historical original and LS-SAE09 both used batch size two; it is not a complete causal explanation of their discrepancy.

## Scientific implications and follow-on

The LS-SAE08 original ACT6 blank predictions were protected by a strict margin certificate `m_min>2*epsilon` for every originally signed physical ±8.00 intervention in all 16 full-test puzzles, as separately documented on merged [PR #132](https://github.com/MoralityLabAI/LoopedTransformers/pull/132). LS-SAE10 independently observes that all original and fresh digit argmaxes remain the same on the two selected sentinels, even with sizeable floating-point differences. This is evidence of stable categorical output under the tested variations, and is **not** evidence of identical hidden trajectories, gradient tensors, or certified floating-point equality.

The next prospective state-transport experiment should register an original-model **within-forward** comparison of clean and edited states and an independent digit/margin certificate on that same execution. Retain exact checkpoint and all source/arm/dose identities, compare across replicated processes as a separately scored sensitivity endpoint, and use a fresh source-only difficulty cohort for claims about reasoning. LS-SAE09 remains an *incomplete* full-cohort result; its original thresholds are unchanged by this diagnostic.
