NEC03Y measures a further property of those **already observed** interventions: the damage each source or disjoint physical sham removal does to task correctness **relative to the same original full-affine restored state**. This retrospective method was prepared after preliminary inspection of the archived outcomes, including additive-root1's 96 full-affine-correct cases becoming incorrect under the originally valid continuous sham. Its statistical summaries are explicitly exploratory, cannot be retroactively registered as an independent confirmatory test, and must preserve the original NEC03X result.
