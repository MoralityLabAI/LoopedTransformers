## Fixed active control panel

At the previously frozen native source candidate visits, the source policy chooses the n highest-ranked **currently active** TopK16 codes, where n is that visit's registered native-neuron count. Each of four SHA256-generated **hash-scrambled source-blind rank permutations** selects n **different** active codes from the same 16-code support, excluding the original source-top set. The original source and hash-control donors are attenuated to their smaller available direct MLP-down output L2 norm on every input/visit. Both gains lie in [0,1].

Every one of the **32 root × hash-control comparisons** passed numerical output-dose equality and the precommitted retained-signal gate (>=80% sites with nonzero shared dose and >=25% median original source-donor dose retained). These controls therefore avoid the low-signal problem seen with the earlier *fixed-list* SAE controls, while still representing a retrospective sham policy family.

### Two reproducible world-specific effects

The source-top active routing was superior to **all four** independently source-blind, same-budget, matched-dose hash controls in **dev-009**, yielding +8 to +41 additional correct predictions. The original bottom-ranked active feature control had yielded +26.

The same source-trained ranking was inferior to **all four** controls in **dev-013**, yielding −6 to −38 fewer correct predictions. The original bottom-ranked active control had yielded −25. This negative world used the source-only nonhub fallback at visits 5 and 7. The hash controls and original source donor all had adequate local direct MLP output strength. This further reduces support for a missing-dose explanation of its negative result.

**dev-008**, despite an original +22 advantage over the source-bottom-ranked control, lost to two of the four hash-ranked active controls (−7, −9). **dev-011**, whose old +73 raw effect was properly excluded for median retained physical dose <25%, produced mixed signs against the four newly physically adequate hash controls (−1, +4, +20, −12). These differences illustrate how the choice of an active, geometrically adequate control changes mechanistic interpretation even without changing the learned model.

## Interpretation

The primary scientific pattern is **direction-dependent causal heterogeneity across source functions and lesion locations**. The learned source-ranked TopK policy can have a consistent positive effect relative to independently selected active features in one Boolean world and a consistent negative effect in another. The source-selected native fallback may contribute, though only one previously eligible negative fallback world is present; its effect cannot be separated from truth-function and focal-position differences here.

The matched physical-dose metric enforces equality of the *magnitude* of the immediate frozen MLP-down output perturbation. It leaves the direction of that perturbation, subsequent looped-memory transport, source-feature attributions and task-specific decision-boundary movement uncontrolled. Matching only a scalar dose is therefore insufficient to identify an intrinsically privileged sparse circuit.

All 32 hash controls are repeated outcomes on the same **eight** already examined worlds. This panel cannot provide 32 independent sham worlds or satisfy EvalMath's **>=59 independent-root** requirement for a 95/95 upper tolerance bound. The earlier NEC-03H v2 ITT world-mean remains **+0.423 percentage points**, below its frozen +2 percentage point minimum; no original primary or null admission criterion was changed. **EvalMath FSI-01: REDESIGN_OR_PILOT_ONLY.**

## Follow-up gym design

The next development mechanism should analyze direct donor **direction alignment** with the downstream decision margin at the selected recurrent visits. On the already examined models, compare physically equal-dose donor projection onto the *local native margin gradient*, the subsequent loop-visit memory-state deviation, and the final source label correctness. Explicitly test whether negative dev-013 donor directions interfere more with its truth function on the already identified focal masks, while the positive dev-009 direction aligns with the teacher's downstream margin. The gradients must be measured on the unperturbed or source-only calibration trajectory and remain separate from heldout selection. Any further interventions on these already viewed worlds are exploratory, with no confirmatory p-values.

Only after such mechanism findings support a fixed, externally checkable hypothesis should a new source-registry and adequately powered **independent teacher-world** sham calibration be preregistered. All 64 existing confirmation-world commitments and original within-world reserved contexts remain unopened.
