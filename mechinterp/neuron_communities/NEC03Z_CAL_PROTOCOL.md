## Frozen calibration selection rule

For each dose and each original world-native teacher arm on the **256 calibration source cases**, count full-affine baseline correctness, originally physically matched controls, sham correct-to-wrong and wrong-to-correct transitions relative to full-affine restoration, plus source correct-to-wrong counts. Correct-to-wrong sham damage rate uses **full-affine-correct AND physically matched sham-valid cases** as denominator. Repairs are recorded independently and do not offset the damage numerator. Null denominator fails eligibility.

The candidate global dose passes only if **all six historically paired-source-eligible worlds and both original teacher arms** satisfy:
- Sham correct-to-wrong rate **≤2%** of matched originally full-affine-correct source cases.
- **≥205/256** originally source cases with complete positive physically dose-matched controls in that arm.
- Within-arm median among admitted original source cases of the *minimum over recurrent sites* of scaled shared physical output32 L2 divided by the source's natural unscaled output32 contribution L2 is **≥0.05**.
- **Task headroom:** at least **two of six eligible independent roots per teacher family** have original full-affine source-training baseline accuracy within the closed range **[60%,95%]**, applied separately to composed and additive families. This guards against reporting source-only calibration over only completely saturated additive teachers.

Choose the **largest** dose among the five that satisfies *all* gates; this choice **must not use source-versus-sham efficacy**. If no dose passes, record the result **STOP_SEAL_ANALYSIS_SOURCE_GROUPS_56_TO_63** and cease evaluation preparation for this family. If a dose passes, record **CALIBRATED_SOURCE_ONLY_AWAIT_SEPARATE_FROZEN_EVALUATION_PROTOCOL**. Even a pass cannot score 56–63 until a **separate prospective Git-locked evaluation protocol and independent-root power plan** are registered. The calibration result, which uses already seen training-source cases, is never population confirmation.
