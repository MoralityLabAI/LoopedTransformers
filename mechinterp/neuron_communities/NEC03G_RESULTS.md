## Pinned EvalMath FSI-01 result

**`REDESIGN_OR_PILOT_ONLY`**. The pinned preflight reported:

- `independence_unverified`: all worlds are structurally distinct, but task populations share one grammar, fixed architecture and finite twelve-bit source.
- `sham_quantile_uncalibrated`: eight independent sham worlds cannot supply a 95/95 upper tolerance bound on the 95th-percentile null; the reference method requires at least **59 independent sham roots**, even when using the maximum.
- `pilot_effect_not_above_mechanistic_variation_floor`: the preregistered 2pp minimum meaningful correctness gain is unachieved after intention-to-treat failure accounting.
- `zero_variance_pilot_power_uninformative`: all administrative root effects are zero, so normal-based extrapolation of a hypothetical 4pp effect would be invalid.
- `confirmation_underpowered`: the planned 64 confirmation roots lack a demonstrated positive resolution estimate under the original root definitions.

These were evaluated with **three planned comparison families**, a **64-root confirmation target** and alpha **0.05**. No confirmation training or scoring occurred. The 48 reserved context quartets per development root are untouched. There is no claim of successful mechanistic mediation, generalization to natural-language models, or evidence against the existence of sparse causal communities.

## Proposed redesign for the remaining development cohort

The first eight development roots and their gates must remain immutable. A **separately versioned NEC-03H protocol** can use unused roots dev-008 through dev-063 for calibration, after testing its changes on already-open development roots and freezing all new selection parameters.

A defensible redesign would:

1. Allow the additive control to use a **precommitted source-only site selector** that does not demand a positive multi-visit co-attribution community. A possible control uses the composed teacher's frozen visit schedule with the additive model's independently selected top-attributed native occurrences, with explicit source-only matching and a separately reported graph-null status.
2. Evaluate a fixed longer native training schedule or a short preregistered curriculum on already-opened development worlds. Report clean model competencies and preserve poor learners in the root-level accounting. Avoid tuning per world.
3. Make the source-ranked active-TopK versus equally active lower-ranked control the **primary feasibility gate**, and treat native/native-sham as distinct falsification contrasts. Compare physical dose retention across individual teacher classes and include dose failure as a zero-valued intention-to-treat primary.
4. Test whether independent newly seeded truth functions yield a positive root-mean effect above the matched-sham floor before allocating 59+ extra sham worlds. Keep exact source hashes and raw 16-state logits.
5. Retain the 64 confirmation-world commitments and prohibit their training until the revised design and EvalMath power gate are frozen in a new PR.

The current failure is useful as a **prospective feasibility audit of the design**. The current negative pilot should remain in the repository and should not be silently replaced by a tuned success.
