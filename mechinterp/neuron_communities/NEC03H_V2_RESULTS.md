The signed root-mean **ITT effect was +0.004231770833**, corresponding to **+0.423 percentage points** over eight new independent development worlds. In integer terms, the positive and negative root effects sum to +26 correct predictions across eight ×768 correlated within-world case measurements. Its scientific interpretation is a world-level mean, and those underlying case counts are only the exact within-world scoring resolution.

The development result falls below the precommitted **+2 percentage point minimum meaningful behavioral gain**. The v2 method improved model competence and source-site availability without producing an adequately strong reproducible causal advantage on this new source population.

## Pinned EvalMath FSI-01 decision

**Decision: `REDESIGN_OR_PILOT_ONLY`.** The preflight recorded these reasons:

- **`independence_unverified`:** world teachers and focal positions are distinct, but all worlds share one Boolean-program grammar, a finite 12-bit input alphabet, and one recurrent model architecture.
- **`sham_quantile_uncalibrated`:** only eight v2 development roots were evaluated, while a 95/95 nonparametric upper sham-tail bound requires at least **59 independent roots** even when using the maximum. Only two v2 roots had all sham controls passing their own feasibility gates.
- **`pilot_effect_not_above_mechanistic_variation_floor`:** the observed +0.423 pp world-mean ITT effect remained below the precommitted +2.0 pp scientific floor.

The pilot included three registered comparison families, alpha 0.05, a planning target effect of +4pp and 64 intended confirmation roots. The output's normal-approximation planning figures remain *descriptive power diagnostics* because the 95/95 matched-sham floor cannot be calibrated from eight roots. There is no confirmation authorization.

## Next research design

Further development should explain why graph-selected communities show different signs across Boolean-world roots and why input-conditioned TopK donors sometimes fail the positive shared-dose or 25% retention floor even after proper attenuation-only matching.

The completed 900-step competence calibration and 4/8 eligible v2 root rate suggest the model-training and source-site stages improved, while the physical-availability and causal specificity stages remain the binding limits. The negative dev-013 fallback effect is particularly important: it measures a case where the lower-ranked active-feature control outperformed source-ranked active features under a matched site and dose.

The remaining unused dev-016..063 cohort contains **48 worlds**. Combined with v2's eight already evaluated worlds this would yield 56 v2 roots, which remains below the 59-root requirement for independently calibrated 95/95 sham tails. Any expansion of the development-world generator must use a separate, newly committed source-population registry and preserve all 64 original confirmation-world commitments. Training them requires a revised, independently justified preflight and fresh locked protocol.

The next causal experiment should retain a fixed primary, run explicit native- and SAE-matched nulls with enough independent task families to calibrate the sham floor, and record sign heterogeneity at the Boolean-function and focal-position level. The possibility of sparse circuits in larger pretrained language models remains a separate research question.
