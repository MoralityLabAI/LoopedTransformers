## Mechanistic target

Ask whether two source-feature pairs are jointly necessary despite either pair being removable alone. This is a redundancy/complementarity hypothesis; SAE coactivation itself is only a way to prepare candidates.

Four active source features form disjoint pairs A and B. Four other active codes form a disjoint geometrically matched control split into C and D. Candidate memberships and matching are chosen from positive original source codes and frozen 128x32 projected SAE decoder geometry, without new model outcomes.

At a single registered recurrent visit v, preserve the native source-trained teacher, original source-hub gates and full SAE-affine donor. Run all original 12 shared-weight recurrent passes.

Factorial arms: baseline; remove A; remove B; remove A+B; remove C; remove D; remove C+D. A/B share the group attenuation factor; C/D share the matched sham attenuation factor. Match combined four-code output L2, pair-relative physical norms and interpair angle. Sham direction must be distinguishable. Invalid cases are identical no-op arms, kept in denominator.

Delayed rescue: after removing A+B at visit v, inject its original source 32D projected direction as a memory correction AFTER whole block visit v+1. Compare with equal-dose 32D matched sham-direction injection at the same visit. At v=11, both later-rescue arms perform zero further injection and remain counted. Exact same-visit addback A+B reproduces full-affine donor by construction; it is only an implementation identity control, excluded from causal rescue evidence.

## Functional estimator

For each original source-case binary misclassification loss L:

    J_group = L(remove AB) - L(remove A) - L(remove B) + L(baseline)
    J_sham  = L(remove CD) - L(remove C) - L(remove D) + L(baseline)

The matched-sham-adjusted interaction is J_group - J_sham. A positive result indicates excess task error from removing both pairs beyond losses from the single pair removals, at the registered physical doses. Matched geometry alone cannot guarantee a full nonlinear behavioral null.
