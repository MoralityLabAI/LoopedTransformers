# NEC-03R: source-only clean-gap and sparse-donor recoverability

**Pre-outcome protocol.** The eight previously source-trained five-input Boolean worlds dev5-000 through dev5-007, each with separately seeded composed and additive model arms, are the complete original cohort. The native and sparse autoencoder checkpoints remain exactly frozen from NEC03O A/B. The experiment evaluates only original **training-source groups 8 through 15**, eight complete groups times 32 focal masks = **256 already trained source histories per arm**. It performs zero optimization.

## All-root denominator and source-specific intervention

All **eight world roots** and **16 model arms** remain in the source-only denominator, including the original dev5-003 additive missing-site arm and dev5-005 composed under-90%-source-accuracy model. For the one primary comparison, retain original paired-admitted root indices 0,1,2,4,6,7 and predeclare root indices 3,5 as zero in the **eight-root** ITT mean. Every complete source group consists of all 32 five-input focal masks. None of the 24 development-evaluation or 24 reserved groups per world, any of the 88 recruited new roots' evaluation data, 32 embargoed roots or 64 old four-input confirmation roots may be scored.

The original frozen native recurrent graph/fallback selects visits and nodes; its graph hubs stay live at selected visits, with nonhubs masked for the hubs-only baseline. Every donor comes from the same input and recurrent visit's frozen clean 64D GELU activation, added back to the hubs-only trajectory through the same native MLP-down prehook. Full 12-visit nonlinear logits are scored. The original source-only 40/40 fit/rank split determines the 128-feature TopK16 basis and n source-ranked active codes at each selected visit.

Ten **predeclared variants** are: clean checkpoint, hubs-only lesion, full native clean nonhub donor, full SAE affine reconstruction masked to nonhubs, sum of all sixteen active SAE contributions masked to nonhubs, original source-ranked n-code donor, source-ranked n-code donor attenuated to the exhaustive angular sham dose, exhaustive disjoint equal-n active-code angular sham, source-ranked donor attenuated to a hash-active random control dose, and its deterministic hash-selected disjoint active-code control.

The SAE affine reconstruction uses decoder(codes)*source_std + source_mean, including decoder bias; sixteen-code contribution-only donor excludes bias and normalization offset. Full native and full SAE nonhub donors use their **natural physical output dose** and are descriptive recovery ceilings, rather than dose-matched mechanistic null controls. The original sparse n-code source direction may have hub-coordinate contributions, preserving its historical policy.

## Strong matched sham, random control and failed-case routing

For each source input and selected visit, take the n highest source-ranked **positive active** codes from the sixteen-code TopK support. Search **every n-code subset** of the remaining 16−n active features, minimizing absolute 32D cosine gap to the source direction against the hubs-only clean-chosen margin gradient at the native post-MLP-down output. Source and selected sham donor vectors are projected by the frozen 32D MLP-down weight and attenuated to the smaller physical L2 norm, never amplified. Source angular control requires a cosine gap at most **0.25**, positive signal and gradient and direct physical dose match within **1%**.

The independent random control uses a SHA256 hash-index into the same deterministic sorted disjoint active-code subset family, salted with **NEC03R-frozen-disjoint-hash-active-source-v1** and original world SHA, teacher arm, source-group ID, focal mask and recurrent visit. It is physically equalized to its source donor with the same attenuation rule and has no angle requirement. Ground-truth task labels are consulted **only after** the entire source/control routing has been determined.

When a source input lacks exactly sixteen positive active codes, a usable gradient, positive physical donor dose or a matched control **at any selected visit**, both donors for that comparison are set to zero across all selected visits. The two comparison trajectories then coincide with hubs-only, producing a fixed zero signed paired effect. Source and random null availability are tracked separately. No input, site, model, root or alternative candidate may be dropped after outcome inspection. An arm without any source candidate has no intervention and its availability indicators are false.

## Prespecified measurements and primary result

Each model arm logs all ten modes' 256 original-source correctness outcomes, signed clean-minus-hubs gap and signed recovery fractions for every donor (conditional on a **positive** clean-hubs gap). Fractions may be negative or exceed one and are descriptive; log null when the signed gap is nonpositive. For every site and input, record the exact source/sham/random TopK IDs and exhaustive candidate count, angular cosine gap, positivity and dose-matching coverage, natural 32D output doses, attenuation gains, **64D SAE affine relative reconstruction error**, 32D physical nonhub error, missing source-code budget and full nonlinear recurrent-memory drift. Root-level statistics remain the unit for inference.

The **single primary exploratory source-only mechanistic contrast** is the composed model's ranked source versus exhaustive angle-matched sham correct-count difference, minus the same correct-count difference in the paired additive model, divided by **256** within each source-admitted world. The original two source-ineligible worlds contribute zero. The primary macro estimate is the mean of these eight registered root differences. The minimum scientifically meaningful effect is the existing **+0.02 absolute correctness fraction**, with one primary comparison.

Native restoration and SAE/full-code recovery ceilings are diagnostics of lost state; their unattenuated physical dose differs from the strict source-versus-sham comparison. Hash-active controls are an additional descriptive physical null. If the source-only primary effect is below the registered 2-point floor or does not exceed an independently defensible sham floor and root-resolution threshold, **stop expansion to new development-evaluation or confirmation cohorts**. This source-only study is in-sample development engineering and never a population causal mediation confirmation.
