# KITE cross-game native adjudication pilot

Target repositories are public, but direct research-branch creation returned GitHub integration 403 twice. This additive cross-repository workflow in LoopedTransformers PR 405 checks out the pinned engines read-only. No upstream code, policy, messages, pacts or live orders change. Native scripts here are equivalent offline development adapters to the earlier standalone ports, with smaller fixed candidate rosters and a scale/belief audit.

DIP: two movement phases, three focal pairs per phase, up to four legal bundles per actor, two declared fixed third-party worlds (hold/pressure). One adjudicated movement plus immediate disband retreat. Shared board across pairs; all roots descend from one initial board. Do not treat pairs, cells or spring/fall as independent statistical roots.

SING: two initial random seeds, turns 1/2 with no prior orders, two focal pairs, up to four move/pass bundles per actor, two third-party worlds (pass/pressure). Only native engine action resolution, no headless enforcement, diplomacy or private knowledge. Same initial seed per cell; action-dependent RNG consumption can differ. Independent experimental units at most two seeds. All tests development/exploratory.

Each utility is owned+occupied centers/nodes divided by twice the map size; the natural global payoff scale can make 0.2 uninformative. Report this global scale and a separate diagnostic local scale (per-player affine normalization across all candidate cells and declared worlds, constant payoff becomes zero). This changes the numeric approximation meaning; a local threshold is relative to the observed action/world utility range. It may be much stricter; exact certificates are scoped to that normalization and declared rosters. Common equal beliefs vs hypothetical asymmetric 3:1/1:3 beliefs, not actual Storyworld predictions. World-specific audit checks the expected-game repaired profile against each declared world. No robust guarantee outside enumerated worlds. No aggregation to global multiplayer equilibrium.

All three arms use identical prior and 2*depth solver matrix products: full history, fixed multiscale updates/full readout, identical multiscale updates/direct readout. Additional exact direct verification and repair work not included in that count; native adjudication costs separate. Compare mean regret and certificates at frozen depths 0/8/16/32/64. Candidate cap is intentionally low and payoff is a tactical proxy. All raw native matrices, cell receipts, profiles and reports uploaded to Actions artifacts; no trained router or BPD. Native runs must pass before claiming engine replay.

Follow-up: use real saved movement phases and native headless sessions with legal coordinated bundles; enforce pact constraints through the harness, introduce actor-specific evidence-based Storyworld beliefs, and include third-party unilateral deviations before considering a global claim. Evaluation cluster is independent game/campaign, not candidate matrix cells. No live policy activation.

## Seven-player pure deviation audit

In addition to the pairwise games, evaluate two frozen pure joint profiles (hold/pass and deterministic pressure) per board/seed/turn, against all unilateral alternatives in the fixed legal roster of every player. At most 1+sum_i(|A_i|-1) native adjudications per joint profile, reusing the incumbent cell. This gives the exact restricted pure-profile regret on a common frozen deterministic engine seed, across all participating powers. It is not a certificate for the pairwise solver's mixed distribution. Report absolute integer utility gains alongside the global normalized threshold; an exact restricted pure Nash flag requires zero profitable deviations. Omitted legal bundles, future turns, private information, pacts and new RNG seeds remain out of scope. Neither pairwise nor this pure-profile audit licenses live execution. All cell receipts and full rosters are retained in the native artifact. Added before reading cross-game test results.

## KITE-JOINT-01: sparse independent mixed-profile verifier

For action counts a_i and nonzero support counts s_i, enumerate the support Cartesian product and each outside-support action against all opposing support tuples. The disjoint pieces have size N=prod_i s_i + sum_i (a_i-s_i) prod_{j!=i} s_j. Any missing or duplicate cell, invalid payoff range, missing player, zero-total strategy or budget breach rejects admission. Exact BigInt weighted sums verify all unilateral deviations, including in-support actions; no 0.2 kite-repair theorem for seven players. Native receipt state hashes are engine signatures, not full-state or oracle-authentication proofs. Independent dense-tensor reference passes 600 generated small cases and 1,800 player regrets. A seven-player four-action roster needs 22 cells for pure, 72 for two mixed players of support two, 1,024 for seven support-two players, or 16,384 dense.

Native development pilot lifts raw depth-32 common-belief, locally normalized two-player candidate profiles for FRANCE/GERMANY and HEGEMON/STATE into the fixed all-hold/pass background. Compare three existing solver arms, their top-two support compressions, and pure anchor; other five players stay pure for candidate generation but are all permitted unilateral deviations in verification. Each board's fixed roster retains focal conditional actions and third-party pure-audit alternatives. One union of required cells across seven candidates, hard budget 256 per board. No adaptive use of test readout to choose the depth or other pair within this new code; TheySing pairwise results already exposed, so this is development only. Roster equality and state signature checked, fixed seed for TheySing. All seven-player regrets use actual re-adjudicated integer utilities and both global and shared observed-range local scales. Local range is measured over this union, not the full tensor. No guarantee for omitted actions or future turns.

Truncation of a distribution to retained support and renormalization changes total variation by exactly discarded mass d_i. For independent products, full and opponent TV are 1-prod(1-d_j) over all players and over j!=i. Since each normalized payoff lies in [0,1], |r_i(x')-r_i(x)| <= min(1, TV_full+TV_opponents). The native audit independently checks this inequality with rational cross-products for every original/truncated pair and every player. This does not guarantee exact 0.2 after truncation; recheck the actual profile. Native results pending.
