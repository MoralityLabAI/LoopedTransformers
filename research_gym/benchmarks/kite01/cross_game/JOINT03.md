# KITE-JOINT-03: separate exploratory workspace and certified incumbent

Development-only protocol. 64 new seeded seven-player games from the four existing generator families, seeds disjoint from JOINT-02, randomized pure initial actions to reduce the previous coordination ceiling. No independent sealed test; generator family reused. All arms cap unique payoff cells at 256 and BR steps at 40. No matched wall time or FLOPs. Exact regret over declared action rosters. No guarantee of finding an equilibrium.

BR continues the largest-current-regret player's pure best response until cycle, exact equilibrium, budget or 40 steps; retains best exact profile by lexicographic (maximum regret, sum regret). Hybrid has identical BR workspace but reserves 64 cells; upon four non-improving BR steps, cycle or reserve boundary, spend remaining budget on one return-only sweep from the best profile (unilateral pure and three modal/best-response blends, <=2 mixed players). Resume the original BR workspace if budget remains. Hybrid-prune adds signed-gain interval lower-bound rejection of return-only candidates strictly worse than the incumbent maximum. Partial candidates never become outputs or exploratory states. Query up to eight missing cells at a time in descending max absolute signed coefficient; ties use fixed tuple ordering. All exact certificates rechecked from stored cell receipts. Rejected completion cell counts sum over proposals and can overlap; not a measured unique-cell saving.

Primary development metric mean exact maximum regret; report paired wins/losses, exact and .05/.2 threshold counts, actual cells, stop reasons, all histories. This is a fixed post-JOINT-02 design, not an FSI-confirmed hypothesis. Native adaptive application will use the same search policy with native adjudicators and separate receipts. This source does not modify live game policies.
