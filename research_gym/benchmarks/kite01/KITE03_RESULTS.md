## Update/readout separation (descriptive)

Both uniform arms use the same fixed bank for best-response updates, on the same roots, with identical deterministic route histories. Only the final readout differs. Exact readout is externally scored and never feeds back into dynamics. Returning the full history instead of the instantaneous bank reduces mean regret by .07960934, .08920349 and .09002675 at depths 16,32,64, respectively; paired wins/losses 108/12, 108/12, 109/11. At depth 32 the held-out ring family improves 24/24, from .22199802 to .04683513 mean regret, with certificate counts 7/24 -> 24/24. This is an observed readout intervention on a fixed solver, not a learned or mechanistically confirmed result. Primary uniform vs full also changes updates, so cannot isolate readout alone.

The readout regret need not improve monotonically; no convergence proof. BPD implications: model the short-horizon mixture as an update workspace and the full-history distribution as a separate decision state. Keep an exact readout certificate independent of both. Do not identify this algorithmic effect with causal neural components.
