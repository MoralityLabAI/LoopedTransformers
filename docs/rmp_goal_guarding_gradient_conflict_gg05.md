# RMP-GG-05: Gradient conflict versus finite-step curvature across recurrent depth

**Registered November 12, 2026, before any GG-05 results are computed.** Base source: LoopedTransformers main at \`15512fb3b85c988442372c242317e82fe726372e\`. GG-04 and GG-04B supplied previous real-SGD measurements, including a 35/48 seed-17 sign reversal when the same report update was judged with four-cue versus six-cue gold cross entropy. Those prior observations motivate this causal-mechanism audit and must remain explicitly distinguished from a fresh confirmatory test.

## Question and mechanism to distinguish

For a native recurrent task model with parameter vector \(\theta\), the native GG-04 one-step reported-label update direction for a four-cue query \(x\) and reported action \(a\in\{C,X\}\) is
\[
g_a(x)=\operatorname{clip}_{\|\cdot\|_2\le1}\nabla_\theta\ell_{\mathrm{report}}(\theta;x,a).
\]
The physical step is \(\theta_a(\eta)=\theta-\eta g_a\); the registered learning rate is \(\eta=0.05\), independent of the audited gold horizon.

Let \(L_4(\theta),L_6(\theta)\) be CE against independent exact Bayesian gold labels at four and six observations. Define the *wrong-versus-truthful update gap*
\[
B_k(x;\eta)=L_k(\theta-\eta g_X)-L_k(\theta-\eta g_C)
\quad(k\in\{4,6\}).
\]
Positive gap means the flipped report damages that audit more than the truthful report.

The first-order term, computed from **actual autograd** at the same frozen initial model, is
\[
A_k(x;\eta)=-\eta\,\langle \nabla L_k(\theta), g_X-g_C\rangle.
\]
The integrated finite-step curvature residual is
\[
R_k(x;\eta)=B_k(x;\eta)-A_k(x;\eta).
\]
At a smooth Gold loss, \(R_k\) equals the difference of the two pathwise Hessian integrals \(\int_0^\eta(\eta-t)g_a^\top H_k(\theta-tg_a)g_a\,dt\), with a positive sign for \(a=X\) and negative sign for \(a=C\). This equality is algebraic for the specified interventions and does **not** assume a constant Hessian.

A measured cross-depth reversal \(\operatorname{sgn}B_4=-\operatorname{sgn}B_6\) is called **first-order explained** only if the first-order terms have the corresponding two nonzero signs. Otherwise classify as **finite-step residual implicated**. Report every ambiguity and zero cell; assign no sign to magnitudes at or below \(10^{-7}\). Do not claim causal self-awareness or deceptive optimization from any outcome.

## Frozen numerical intervention

For each of 48 queries and either true or flipped report label, compute a fresh native task-model **PyTorch** backward gradient at the frozen base weights; global norm clip **1.0** as in GG-04B, including the whole parameter vector. Preserve each raw gradient norm, clipped norm, gradient cosine between the two report directions, and the original task-model parameter hash. Compute all four sealed gold gradients *once per model seed* at the frozen baseline by independent autograd.

Evaluate the actual native CE **after an SGD optimizer step** from the same frozen base for each action at the registered grid \(\eta\in\{0.0125,0.025,0.05\}\). At every step score all four gold panels without further updating. For each query/horizon/panel/LR compute \(B_k,A_k,R_k\); preserve the raw truth and flip post-update loss. A second independent reference in tests performs ordinary one-step SGD without using the decomposition helper. Treat model weights and reporting gradients as fixed across \(\eta\).

The three rates are a *predeclared sensitivity curve*. The primary sign classification uses \(\eta=0.05\) on the **fresh** pair of gold panels, with the \(10^{-7}\) zero margin. Report intermediate-rate disagreements and the curvature residual ratio \(R_k(0.025)/R_k(0.05)\) only where the denominator is numerically identifiable; the expected small-step quadratic ratio is approximately \(1/4\). Do not force a second-order fit when residuals are close to floating-point resolution.

Report the signed cosine
\[
c_{46}=\frac{\langle \nabla L_4,\nabla L_6\rangle}{\|\nabla L_4\|\|\nabla L_6\|},
\]
and the fraction of report directions \(g_X-g_C\) that project with opposed signs on the two gold gradients. This distinguishes **global objective-gradient conflict** from the extent to which specific report directions experience that conflict. Neither global gradient cosine nor opposite first-order projections alone establishes the observed *finite* update sign reversal.

For any query with strictly opposed four/six-cue gap signs, report the unique gold-mixture weight where a convexly mixed protected objective changes the sign of the update gap:
\[
\lambda^*_{\rm finite}=\frac{B_4}{B_4-B_6},
\quad
\lambda^*_{\rm linear}=\frac{A_4}{A_4-A_6}
\]
when each denominator is identifiable, with valid weights in \((0,1)\). This mixture is an *auditor-defined diagnostic*, not an optimizer adjustment chosen by the agent.

## Success criteria and falsifiers

For **each seed** on the fresh scoring panels, report:
1. Number of nonzero finite-sign cross-depth reversals and proportion of 48 queries, with zero/ambiguous counts;
2. Number of these reversals exactly predicted by both first-order projection signs and number requiring finite residual influence, and the signed gold-gradient cosine;
3. Per-depth sign agreement, median and max absolute residual, and distribution of residual / first-order magnitude;
4. Sign stability across the predeclared 0.0125, 0.025, 0.05 learning-rate grid, and critical mixture weights when identified;
5. Identical statistics on the previously observed scoring panels, labeled historical replication.

A **first-order-conflict-supported** two-seed result requires at least **half** of the fresh-panel reversal queries for *each seed* to have both signs explained by first order and at least **eight** fresh-panel reversal queries per seed. A **curvature-sensitive** result requires at least **eight** fresh-panel reversals for which one or both first-order signs disagree with the measured finite step, in at least one seed. Other cases are mixed or no-reversal. These are exploratory mechanisms on two trained toy models, not population-level statistical claims.

The experiment's basic instrumental pass depends on exact-source seed/model matches, proper clipping, frozen weights, independent SGD reference, exact gold label hashes, finite-step identities, and all numerical/unit tests. Scientific gate failures remain results with full receipts.

## Scope and planned successor

This task tests *why a wrong-label gradient changes the task loss differently at different recurrent inference depths*. No agent chooses the SGD learning rate or sees the sealed future gold; no new controller or active disclosure is trained. The test provides the applied gradient geometry needed to design a later **same-weights meta-learning / training-awareness** protocol, whose evidence claims must start from separate preregistration. GG-05 cannot substantiate spontaneous Mythos-like training awareness.
