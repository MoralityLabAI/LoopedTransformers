LIMITS
Only two exploratory development cases from the same signed-family/source-program stratum; no population inference.
Available geometry consists of hidden-state norms, perturbation L2 trajectories, and full-vocabulary readout-response norms. Raw hidden tensors were not saved by NF4; cross-backend state angles or CKA cannot be computed.
Random and structured perturbations are matched initially, not at every propagated depth; normalized response gains are descriptive and are not the prospective time-local dose-matched causal control.
Fresh streamed BF16 is matched on candidate shape/software; historical 96-case BF16 data use different case identities and are not paired here.
Bit-exact resident-versus-streamed BF16 was verified on a small pinned-code fixture; the full checkpoint was run streamed on the 4 GiB GPU.
Rank KL is over the normalized 64-program scores, not the full vocabulary.
